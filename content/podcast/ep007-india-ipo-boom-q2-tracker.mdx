# Show Notes

The Indian IPO market had its strongest quarter in history — and the SME board is running even hotter. This is our quarterly tracker: what listed, what popped, what got pulled, and what the signals say about the next quarter.

- **Mainboard numbers**: Funds raised, average listing gains, and the pipeline of filings.
- **The SME phenomenon**: Subscription multiples of 500x+ and why SEBI is watching.
- **The risks**: Valuation froth, anchor-holder exits, and what a rate cut cycle would change.

---

# Key Points

1. **Q2 2026 in numbers**
   - Mainboard IPOs raised over ₹1.2 lakh crore in the first half of 2026 — a record pace.
   - Average listing-day gain moderated to ~25%, down from the frothier prints of late 2025.

2. **The SME board is the story within the story**
   - SME IPO subscription multiples repeatedly crossed 500x, driven by retail speculation and scarcity.
   - SEBI tightened SME listing norms — tighter disclosure, higher net-worth criteria for directors.

3. **What changed this quarter**
   - FII flows turned positive again after two volatile quarters; domestic mutual funds remain the marginal price-setter.
   - Large offer-for-sale components suggest founders and VCs are using the window to exit.

4. **Looking to Q3**
   - The pipeline includes multiple ₹10,000 crore+ issuances — quick commerce, fintech, and auto-ancillaries.
   - Watch the grey-market premium for sentiment; watch the anchor lock-ins for supply.
