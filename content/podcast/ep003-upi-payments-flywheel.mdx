# Show Notes

Twenty billion transactions a month. That is the scale UPI has reached — roughly 8,000 transactions per second, every second of every day. This episode summarises our research note on what the flywheel looks like underneath.

- **The network effect**: Why more users beget more merchants, and more merchants beget more users.
- **The monetisation gap**: UPI is free to users — so where does the money actually get made?
- **The second act**: Credit on UPI, UPI Lite, and the international expansion of the rail.

---

# Key Points

1. **Scale and structure**
   - UPI crossed 20 billion monthly transactions, processing over ₹30 lakh crore in value.
   - The system runs on a hub-and-spoke model: NPCI operates the rail, banks and apps fight for the customer.

2. **Who actually earns**
   - The interchange on UPI is zero for most P2P and small P2M payments — a deliberate policy choice.
   - The economics live in adjacent layers: lending, insurance, ads, and merchant services.

3. **Credit on UPI is the frontier**
   - Pre-sanctioned credit lines on UPI could be the largest financial inclusion lever since the PMJDY account boom.
   - Risk: unsecured small-ticket lending growing faster than underwriting models can adapt.

4. **Investor takeaways**
   - Payments are a distribution game now, not a fee game — value accrues to whoever owns the customer relationship.
   - Watch the credit cycle for signals on fintech health.
