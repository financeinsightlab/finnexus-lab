# Show Notes

Zepto has filed confidentially for what could be the largest quick-commerce IPO India has ever seen. In this solo analysis, we unpack the story behind the headline ₹20,000 crore number.

- **The filing**: Revenue run-rate, gross margin trajectory, and the dark-store footprint at the time of filing.
- **The bull case**: Category leadership in 10-minute delivery, improving take rates, and a cash position that funds growth without dilution.
- **The bear case**: Marketing intensity, competition from Blinkit and Instamart, and whether "quick" is a moat or a commodity.

This episode is for anyone tracking Indian consumer internet — investors, operators, and students of the space.

---

# Key Points

1. **The headline numbers**
   - Zepto's annualised gross merchandise value crossed ₹30,000 crore ahead of the filing.
   - The IPO is expected to raise ₹20,000–25,000 crore, with a mix of fresh issue and offer-for-sale.

2. **Unit economics at filing**
   - Contribution margin turned positive across mature dark stores — but company-level EBITDA remains negative.
   - Delivery cost per order has fallen as store density rises; the bet is density keeps compounding.

3. **Competitive reaction**
   - Blinkit (Zomato) and Swiggy Instamart are both scaling aggressively; a price war around IPO timing is the key risk.
   - A successful listing would reset valuation benchmarks for the entire category.

4. **Investor takeaways**
   - Watch the IPO's price band vs. last private round — the spread tells you who holds the leverage.
   - Post-listing, the real question is same-store contribution margin, not GMV growth.
