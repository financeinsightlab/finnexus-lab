## 1. Executive Assessment

### 1.1 What Kunwar Analytics actually is [VERIFIED]

A single Next.js 16 / React 19 / Prisma–Postgres application that simultaneously tries to be **five
products**:

1. **Research & content publication** — research reports, insights, case studies, projects, data-lab
   exhibits, podcast, finance-terms glossary (MDX in `content/` + DB CMS).
2. **A financial-calculator suite** — 16 registered tools (`lib/tools-registry.ts`), from DCF/CCA to
   tokenomics and CPM.
3. **A learning platform** — PGDM curriculum (subjects→lectures→lessons→quizzes), study materials,
   courses, assessments, certificates, placement prep.
4. **A market-intelligence surface** — sector trackers, "contrarian radar", a timestamped
   predictions board + public ledger, data-freshness SLAs.
5. **A personal-brand consultancy storefront** — services, enterprise, speaking, résumé, contact,
   manual-UPI subscriptions (PRO/ELITE).

Plus a genuinely large **admin/CMS/promotions/payments back office** (50 Prisma models, 65 API
handlers). The engineering quality of the bones is well above typical solo projects: typed domain
stores, server-side entitlements, provenance-stamped data code, honest in-product disclaimers, CI
with typecheck/lint/test/build + Lighthouse budgets.

### 1.4 Honest one-paragraph verdict

Kunwar Analytics is an unusually broad, craft-heavy solo build with two assets that are genuinely
hard to copy — a validated financial-modelling engine library (on branch) and a credible,
methodology-led content/learning corpus — wrapped in positioning too broad to sell, monetised
through a payment flow that cannot scale past individuals, and currently **switched off**. The
fastest path to revenue is not more surface area; it is choosing **one wedge** (India-first
founder/analyst modelling work, sold as subscriptions + paid model packs/reports + services),
shipping the branch, wiring real payments and saved-work persistence, and demoting everything else
to supporting content. Details in §4, §9, §10.
