## 📘 Concepts

### 12.1 The Tribunal Protocol — five beats

Every file at this desk gets the same five beats, in order:

1. **FRAME** the fact pattern: what's the instrument/item, whose books, what date?
2. **CITE** the governing standard (and the fallback hierarchy from IA2 when none governs)
3. **CLASSIFY**: which category/element/stage/bucket does it land in?
4. **MEASURE**: the number, with assumptions declared
5. **DISCLOSE**: what the notes must confess (level, sensitivity, judgment)

The protocol beats memorization because real files never arrive labeled. Your capstone grade is whether the five beats come out in order under pressure.

### 12.3 The OCI-or-P&L Sorting Drill — trial canon

## 💪 Exercises — The Interview Forge

Say these out loud, 45 seconds each, citations first:

1. "Explain ECL to a risk committee in five sentences, ending in the coverage ratio formula."
2. "Your client wants L1 treatment for a thinly traded bond because 'it has a Bloomberg page.' Rule."
3. "Board asks: can our strategic equity stake's OCI ever hit profit? Answer and close the file."
4. "Walk me through an equity reconciliation — why does every line carry a tax shadow?"
5. "A €-payable and a €-machine both come off an import. Why does only one of them bleed at year-end?"
6. "Deferred tax assets: current or non-current — and who decided?"
7. "When does a company present a third balance sheet?"
8. "What's the fallback when no Ind AS covers the transaction?"

### ✅ Selected answers

1. "Ind AS 109 provisions losses it EXPECTS, not losses it has seen. Performing loans carry 12-month ECL at origination — Stage 1. Significant risk increase flips them to lifetime ECL — Stage 2, presumed at 30 days past due, rebuttable only with evidence. Credit-impaired loans stay lifetime with interest on the net book — Stage 3. Estimates are PD × LGD × EAD, macro-conditioned, discounted at EIR. Coverage = allowance ÷ gross book, reported stage-wise."
2. "Rejected. Level 1 is quoted prices in ACTIVE markets for IDENTICAL instruments — a quote page is not market activity. Stale/thin trades push the bond to L2 (comparable-yield pricing) or L3 (own assumptions), and the level follows the lowest significant input, with the transfer disclosed."
3. "Never through profit. The FVOCI-equity election is the one-way door: gains and losses live in OCI-A permanently and on disposal move within equity. Dividends reach P&L; capital gains never do. File closed — the election's price is paid for precisely this discipline."
4. "Each bridge line re-measures an asset or liability, and every temporary book-versus-tax-base difference creates deferred tax under Ind AS 12: ECL −40 creates a DTA (+10 at 25%), the land uplift +300 creates a DTL (−75). Equity moves net of tax because the tax base didn't move with the accounting."
5. "Both were measured at 88 on day one. The machine is a non-monetary asset at cost — history freezes it at ₹88.0L. The payable is a monetary promise in a foreign unit — Ind AS 21 retranslates it at closing: ₹3.5L of pain to P&L. Frozen assets, living liabilities."
6. "Always non-current — Ind AS 1's classification canon, deliberately cycle-independent, decided by the standard and not by anyone's reversal forecast."
7. "On retrospective policy changes, restatements, or reclassifications — the opening comparative balance sheet goes on the face of the statements so the change's first-order effects cannot hide in a note."
8. "Ind AS 8's hierarchy: standards addressing similar issues; then the Framework's definitions, recognition criteria and measurement concepts; then recent pronouncements of other framework-similar setters and accepted practice — with the judgment itself disclosed."

(a) both retranslate: machine ₹91.5L, loss ₹3.5L
(b) machine frozen at ₹88.0L (non-monetary at cost); payable retranslated to ₹91.5L with ₹3.5L exchange loss in P&L — para 46A-style capitalization is unavailable to Ind AS adopters

**Q2.** FinBerry's regulator-ordered migration of ₹50cr from Stage 1 (1.0%) to Stage 2 (5.0%) costs the quarter:
(a) ₹50cr
(b) ₹2.0cr — the incremental-rate shortcut: exposure × the allowance-rate gap lands straight in P&L
(c) zero — stages are disclosure labels
(d) ₹0.5cr

**Q3.** VedaTextiles' bridge lands Ind AS opening equity at:
(a) ₹1,200cr
(b) ₹1,413cr — 1,200 + 300 − 40 + 18 − 75 + 10, each line carrying its standard citation and its tax shadow
(c) ₹1,338cr
(d) ₹1,488cr

### ✅ Answers

1. **(b)** — frozen assets, living liabilities; election-era carve-outs do not cross the Ind AS bridge.
2. **(b)** — shortcut math: 50 × 4.0pp = ₹2.0cr; the protocol measures the delta, not the drama.
3. **(b)** — build the bridge line by line, tax shadows attached: 1,413.
