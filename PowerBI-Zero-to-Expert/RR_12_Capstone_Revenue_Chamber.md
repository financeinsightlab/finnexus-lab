## 📘 Concepts

### 12.1 The Chamber Protocol — eleven modules, one drill

Step order is sacred: (1) enforceable contract? (2) count the promises (distinct ×2, series); (3) price it (variables + constraint + SFC + non-cash + netting); (4) slice it (SSP evidence-first); (5) clock it (OT criteria → measure with surgery; else PIT on control). Then place the balances (conditionality knife), capitalise costs if the gates pass (RR7), and apply the edge vaults (licenses/agent/warranties/returns; consignment/B&H/fees/options/breakage; POC/onerous). The Chamber does not forgive protocols run out of order: slicing before counting promises is how quarters get restated.

### 12.2 The Timing Trial — PIT/OT sort at speed

Ten exhibits, verdict-only, citation-ready: (i) daily office cleaning — **OT(crit 1)**; (ii) factory-line machine, drop-in install — **PIT at delivery/acceptance**; (iii) tower on customer's land — **OT(crit 2)**; (iv) bespoke automated line with cost+margin termination rights — **OT(crit 3)**; (v) 3-year evolving content library — **OT(access)**; (vi) perpetual static software, no updates promised — **PIT at use-readiness**; (vii) marketplace GMV ₹10L, commission 15% — **₹1.5L agent net, PIT per sale**; (viii) annual SaaS prepaid — **OT, ₹10k/mo drip**; (ix) custom girders in the seller's yard, bill-and-hold fortress passed — **PIT now**; (x) gym joining fee — **NOT a PO, drip with membership (₹1,000/mo canon)**. Read this back in 30 seconds; that cadence IS the exam.

**File 1 — The BundleDesk.** TelcoX: ₹1,000/mo × 24 + "free" phone (SSP ₹18,000; service SSP ₹750/mo). Dealer commission ₹3,000 upfront. Customer bills monthly from activation. Produce: day-1 revenue, monthly revenue, contract asset at month 6, commission treatment.

**File 2 — The TowerBook.** ₹10cr canon tower, year 2 runs to ₹7.0cr cumulative costs with total estimate slippage to ₹10.5cr. Board asks for revenue this year and whether to "wait on the loss."

**File 3 — The ElevatorJob.** ₹5.0cr price; ₹1.0cr costs to date including ₹40L uninstalled lifts; ₹4.0cr total estimate. CFO proposed 25% progress.

**File 4 — The RoyaltyFile.** Franchise deal: ₹50L fixed fee + 4% of franchisee sales; sales history suggests ₹8cr/yr plausible. What licenses now, and when does the 4% ever count?

**File 5 — The ReturnRack.** 100-unit canon quarter, then actual returns hit 6.8% at quarter-end. Bridge the estimate change.

**File 6 — The SigningBonus.** ₹100L paid now for equipment in 2 years at an 8% appropriate rate; delivery scheduled as contracted. Year-1 and year-2 books, then delivery-day entry.

**Why this matters:** six files is one audit fieldwork week — the full machine at speed, with the constraint and the loss law both forced to fire.

**🔑 Lab answers:**
1. Day-1 revenue **₹12,000** (handset slice); monthly **₹500** service; contract asset month-6 ≈ **₹9,000**; commission ₹3,000 capitalize over expected relationship life (≤24 mo + justified renewals) — as incremental obtain-cost (no expedient since the amortization window exceeds 12 months).
2. Revenue: 66.67% cumulative ₹6.667cr → **₹2.667cr this year**; no waiting: expected total loss ₹0.5cr law fires TONIGHT — extra provision **₹0.167cr** (booked −0.333 vs full −0.5), Ind AS 37 through P&L immediately.
3. Reject 25%: adjusted progress (100−40)/(400−40) = **16.67%**; revenue = **₹83.33L + ₹40L zero-margin = ₹1.233cr** — elevators moved to control but delivered no effort.
4. Fixed ₹50L: if it's a right-to-ACCESS brand stream → over-time over the franchise term; static IP/package without ongoing significant-activities → PIT at grant. The 4% sales royalty: **exception applies — recognize only as franchisee sales actually happen** (constraint-to-zero until then; ₹32L/yr estimate stays a footnote until banked).
5. Estimate change flows forward: expected returns were 5 → now 6.8%: additional 1.8 units (₹3,600 revenue reversal-equivalent) trued up: refund liability to ₹13,600, recovery asset to ₹9,520, revenue down ₹3,600, COGS down ₹2,520 — flowed within the next quarter's statements, flagged to trend-watch as the season normalizes.
6. Year 1: contract liability accretes with **interest expense ₹8.0L** (100 × 8%); year 2: **₹8.64L**. Delivery day: revenue **₹116.64L**, liability zeroes, embedded loan paid off by performance.

## 💪 Exercises — The Interview Forge

Say these out loud, step-citations first:

1. "Walk me through the five steps on a ₹24,000 telecom plan in 45 seconds."
2. "Your client books unsigned claim estimates in progress 'to smooth the year.' Rule."
3. "When does a free phone get its own line in the P&L, and for how much?"
4. "Why does Ind AS 37 collide with Ind AS 115 on loss-making jobs?"
5. "Explain a contract asset to a banker using the telecom example, one breath."
6. "When does your marketplace print GMV as revenue? When not?"
7. "Why are software licenses the only happy home of residual pricing, and what ejects a residual?"
8. "A customer paid 2 years early. Revenue isn't ₹100L at delivery — why is it exactly ₹116.64L?"

### ✅ Selected answers

1. "Step 1: enforceable 24-month plan ✓. Step 2: TWO POs — handset, service. Step 3: ₹24,000 total consideration (concessions assessed). Step 4: SSP slices — handset 18/36 → ₹12,000, service 12/36 → ₹12,000. Step 5: handset PIT at activation ₹12,000; service OT ₹500/month; balance trail: contract asset unwinding at ₹500/bill."
2. "Rejected: enforceability is Step 1 — unsigned claims enter at zero with pipeline evidence. Only signed variations become price estimates under Step 3, constraint-attached. Smoothing via claims is exactly the channel the standard closed."
3. "When it's a distinct PO — capable of standalone use and not welded to service — it gets allocated consideration at relative SSP (₹12,000 on the ₹24,000 canon), recognized point-in-time at activation. Free is a price; the allocation finds it."
4. "115 says recognize revenue on the over-time measure; 37 says recognize the TOTAL unavoidable loss the moment it's visible. Operating together: POC keeps booking margin/lost margin by progress, AND the full expected deficit provisions immediately — revenue honesty AND loss honesty in the same quarter."
5. "I delivered a ₹12,000 phone today; the customer pays it back at ₹500/month only if he stays on the plan — my right to that money is CONDITIONED on the plan continuing, so it sits as a contract asset, leaking into real receivables with every bill."
6. "GMV when the platform controls goods before transfer: owns inventory risk, pricing discretion, fulfillment responsibility — principal. Not GMV — rather net commission — when it arranges sales between merchants and buyers: ₹15 on a ₹100 basket, notes carrying GMV for scale."
7. "Because their standalone prices genuinely swing deal-to-deal — highly variable input, making top-down estimation unreliable; residual becomes the honest remainder. Ejected by absurdity: a zero/negative/outlier residual versus market evidence sends you back to lanes 1-2 (adjusted market, cost+margin)."
8. "The ₹100L arrived 2 years early; between receipt and delivery the contract carried an embedded loan: 8% accretion adds ₹16.64L interest expense across 24 months, so the delivery-day revenue is the accreted price ₹116.64L. SFC math: recognition price equals cash-selling equivalent."

## ❓ Quiz

**Q1.** File 3's verdict — elevators inside the ₹5cr job — is:
(a) 25% progress, as the CFO computed
(b) adjusted progress 16.67% with the ₹40L lifts at zero margin — ₹1.233cr cumulative revenue; parked materials aren't effort, and naive cost-to-cost gets surgically corrected
(c) full costs in progress, revenue ₹1.25cr
(d) exclude lifts entirely from all of revenue

**Q2.** The 4% franchise royalty recognizes:
(a) ₹32L/yr from day one on a solid estimate
(b) only as the franchisee's sales actually occur — the sales-or-usage-based royalty exception parks estimates at zero until the trigger; fixed-fee treatment runs its separate use-vs-access test
(c) 50% upfront per the constraint
(d) nothing until audit of the franchisee

**Q3.** TelcoX's month-6 contract asset lands at:
(a) ₹12,000 unchanged
(b) ₹9,000 — ₹12,000 handset slice minus six ₹500 billing slices reclaimed; conditional claims unwind into receivables as bills rise
(c) ₹6,000
(d) ₹0 — it was settled upfront

### ✅ Answers

1. **(b)** — exclusion surgery: 60/360 progress, not 100/400; the canon holds under audit.
2. **(b)** — the exception is absolute: royalty estimates stay at zero until the underlying sales actually occur.
3. **(b)** — 12,000 − 6×500 = 9,000: every bill converts one slice from conditional claim to unconditional receivable.
