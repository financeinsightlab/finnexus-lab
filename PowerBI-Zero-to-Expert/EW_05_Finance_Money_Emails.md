## 📘 5.1 The religion of money emails: FACTS REMOVE EMOTION 🙏

Every money email must carry its **holy trinity** — every single time, even in a one-liner:

```
1. THE NUMBER:     invoice/PO/transaction reference
2. THE AMOUNT:     exact, with ₹ + taxes called out
3. THE DATE:       due date / paid date / validity
```

Miss one, and you get reply-chains. Carry all three, and money moves. Professionals say *"invoice #FF-221 for ₹47,250, due 08-Aug"* — never just "your previous invoice".

## 📘 5.2 Invoice emails — sending money requests professionally 🧾

```
Subject: Invoice #FF-221 — Fresh Farms — ₹47,250 (due 08-Aug-2026)

Dear Mr Mehta,

Please find attached invoice #FF-221 for July supplies:

- Description: fresh produce supply, July 2026 (PO-118)
- Amount: ₹45,000 + 5% GST (₹2,250) = ₹47,250
- Payment terms: Net 30 → due 08-Aug-2026
- Bank details: on the invoice footer (HDFC, IFSC included)

Kindly confirm receipt and the scheduled payment date.
Thank you for your continued association.

Regards,
Kiran Rao · Billing Desk · Fresh Farms
```

✅ Checklist: PDF attached AND referenced · GST/tax split shown · terms (Net 15/30) stated · bank/UPI details present · "confirm receipt + payment date" ask (creates accountability!). Sent as attachment → also quote key numbers in the body (mobile CFOs read bodies, not PDFs!).

## 📘 5.3 The 3-Step Payment Reminder Ladder 🪜🎧

Money chases with structure, not anger. One thread, rising firmness:

```
STEP 1 — GENTLE REMINDER (on due date)
"This is a friendly reminder that invoice #FF-221 for ₹47,250,
dated 09-Jul, falls due today. Kindly arrange the payment at
your earliest convenience."

STEP 2 — FIRM REMINDER (+7 days overdue)
"Invoice #FF-221 for ₹47,250 is now 7 days overdue under our
Net-30 terms. Please release the payment by Friday 15-Aug and
share the UTR number once done. If any issue with the invoice,
reply and we'll resolve it the same day."

STEP 3 — FINAL NOTICE (before escalation)
"Despite earlier reminders, invoice #FF-221 (₹47,250) remains
unpaid as of today. Please treat this as a final reminder before
we escalate per our agreement terms [late-fee/legal clause].
We value our association and request immediate settlement."
```

🎧 **Hear the ladder spoken:** [3-step payment reminders — model voice (Indian English)](english_audio/ew5_payment_reminder_models.mp3) — notice the steady, warm-but-firm tone. Facts carry pressure; tone carries respect.

**The ladder's secrets:** same thread (history visible) · holy trinity EVERY step · each step has a NEW deadline · step 2 adds a dispute escape ("if any issue…") because 30% of late payments are actually errors · step 3 references the AGREEMENT (which is why contracts carry late-fee clauses!).

## 📘 5.4 Payment confirmations & receipts — closing the loop 💸

As payer:
```
Subject: Payment released — Invoice #FF-221, ₹47,250, UTR: UT22H90873

Hi Kiran,
Payment of ₹47,250 against invoice #FF-221 released today via NEFT.
UTR: UT22H90873 · Value date: 08-Aug-2026.
Please confirm receipt once credited.
Regards, Amit
```
As receiver (credit confirmation = relationship gold):
```
Subject: Received with thanks — Invoice #FF-221

Hi Amit, ₹47,250 credited this morning. Receipt attached. Thanks!
Kiran
```

**UTR number** (bank's transaction reference) is the magic word of Indian payments — quote it, hunt with it. Also meet: **proforma invoice** (estimate before work) · **credit note** (the "we owe you back" document) · **statement of accounts** (monthly all-open-invoices list).

## 📘 5.5 Budget & expense approvals — internal money asks 🏛️

```
Subject: Approval request: Power BI certification ₹18,000 (Q3 training budget)

Hi Sneha,

Requesting approval for my PL-300 certification, ₹18,000.

- Budget line: L&D / certifications (₹50K annual head; ₹32K unused)
- Payback: client dashboards currently billed at vendor rate ₹1,800/hr
  vs in-house ₹0 — cert unlocks 2 in-house analysts
- Timing: exam slot Sept 12; fee reimbursable per policy §4.2

Attached: course outline + policy excerpt.
Could you approve by Friday so I can book the early slot?

Thanks, Amit
```

Recipe: **budget line named + ROI in one line + deadline + attachment.** Finance approves LEDGERS, not wishes — speak their line-of-the-ledger language.

## 📘 5.6 Salary: offer queries & negotiation emails (India special 💼)

**Querying an offer (politely, before accepting):**
```
Subject: Query on offer — breakup and joining formalities

Dear Ms Kapoor,
Thank you for the Data Analyst offer — delighted to accept!
Before I return the signed copy, could you share:
1. The detailed CTC breakup (fixed vs variable)
2. HRA / relocation support for the Bengaluru move
3. Whether the 30-day notice at my current role is workable
Thanks again, Amit
```

**Negotiating (never by phone first — put it in writing):**
```
Subject: Counter-proposal: Data Analyst offer — Amit Sharma

Dear Ms Kapoor,

Thank you again for the offer of ₹9.0 LPA. Based on my current
in-hand (~₹62,000) plus bonus and the role's Power BI scope,
I was expecting around ₹10.5 LPA fixed.

If ₹10.5 works, I'm ready to sign this week. If there's flexibility
on joining bonus or a 6-month review instead, happy to discuss.

Either way — excited about the team. Looking forward.

Regards, Amit
```

💪 Negotiation rules: anchor with YOUR number + reason (current comp + scope) · trade alternatives (fixed vs bonus vs early review) · warmth throughout (you'll work with these people!) · deadline for yourself ("this week") shows seriousness · **everything in writing** (recruiters change; inboxes remember).

## 📘 5.7 Quoting numbers in emails — the CFO voice 📐

- **One hero number per email.** ShopKart revenue "up 21% YoY to ₹71.8 lakh" → bold it; the 9 supporting numbers go in the attachment.
- **Round, then point:** "about ₹72 lakh (exact: ₹71,78,556 — sheet attached)".
- **Never a naked price:** price always carries `+ taxes` + `validity` + `scope`:
  "Our quote: ₹1,10,000 + 18% GST, valid till 31-Aug, includes 2 revision rounds."
- **Variance talk:** "Favourable ₹12K vs budget" / "Unfavourable 6% on freight" — pair every variance with the driver (your Finance GL modules trained this!).

Run ShopKart's payment life from invoice to receipt — 5 real emails, one thread where natural:

1. **Receive & process (10 min):** Fresh Farms emails YOU invoice #FF-221 (₹45,000 + 5% GST, Net 30, due 08-Aug). Write your inwards confirmation mail: receipt confirmed, PO match done, payment date scheduled (quote the date), CC finance.

4. **The plot twist (20 min):** now REPLAY history as the vendor — your client ShopKart missed the 08-Aug due date. Write all 3 reminders (gentle on due date, firm at +7, final at +21) in one thread format, every step with holy trinity + new deadline + dispute escape + escalation step at the end. 🎧 Compare against the model clip — match its firm-warm balance.
5. **Salary round (15 min):** mock HR offers ₹8.5 LPA for a junior-analyst role; your current in-hand is ₹55,000 + bonus. Write the counter at ₹9.5 fixed with a joining-bonus alternative. Then role-swap: as HR, respond warmly with a ₹9.0 fix + 6-month review structure counter-counter.

## 💪 Exercises
1. Spot the money-email sin: "Hi, your payment is quite delayed now, please arrange asap. Thanks." — list all missing elements.
2. Write the STEP-1 line ONLY for: invoice #MT-88, ₹1,20,000, Net-15, due tomorrow.
3. As the payer, write the payment-confirmation mail for invoice MT-88 (choose a UTR + value date).
4. You're quoting dashboard development: 3 weeks of work, ~₹2.4 lakh. Write the ONE price sentence with taxes, validity and scope.
5. Below-market offer (₹6 LPA) reached you when you expected ₹8 LPA; write the counter-proposal paragraph (anchor + reason + alternative + warmth).

### ✅ Selected answers
- Ex 1: no invoice number, no amount, no dates, no thread reference, "asap" (no deadline), no dispute escape, tone drifting toward annoyed — a masterclass in what NOT to send.
- Ex 2: "Gentle reminder: invoice #MT-88 for ₹1,20,000 falls due tomorrow (22-Jul). Kindly schedule the release at your convenience. Happy to help if any invoice details are needed."
- Ex 4: "Our quote: ₹2,40,000 + 18% GST, valid till 31-Aug-2026, covering the full 3-week build, 2 revision rounds and a 30-day post-delivery support window."
- Ex 5: "Thank you for the ₹6 LPA offer — genuinely excited about the analytics work. Based on my current package and the SQL/Power BI scope discussed, I was expecting around ₹8 LPA fixed. If base is capped, a joining bonus or a 90-day performance review could bridge it. Happy to jump on a quick call!"

## ❓ Quiz
1. What's the "holy trinity" of money emails and why does it end 80% of disputes before they begin?
2. Walk the 3-step reminder ladder: what changes between step 1 and step 3 — and what NEVER changes?
3. Why does step 2 of the ladder include "if there's any issue with the invoice, reply"?
4. In salary negotiation, why put your counter in writing instead of first calling?

### ✅ Answers
1. Invoice/PO reference + exact amount (tax split) + exact date. Disputes breed in vagueness ("that payment?") — naming all three makes the transaction uniquely identifiable, so there's nothing to argue about.
2. Changes: politeness (friendly → firm → final), deadline (due today → new date), consequence (none → escalation per agreement). Never changes: the same thread, the holy trinity, professional warmth. Structure escalates; manners don't.
3. Because a big share of "late payments" are actually blocked on errors (wrong GST, missing PO). The escape clause recovers the money faster than pressure would — and keeps the relationship.
4. Inboxes remember; phone calls evaporate. Written counters keep your anchor, reasons and alternatives exact under re-reading — and survive recruiter handovers.
