## 📘 Concepts

### 8.1 The brief → the architecture
Client DNA: income ≥ ₹3.5L/yr • horizon 6y+ • moderate nerve • taxable at slab. Mandate translation: dial target ModD ≈ 5–5.5 (horizon-matched), sovereign spine ≥ 60% of risk-free weight, credit muscle modest with FI6 checklist + ≤6% single-name exception documented, buffer small. **Desk canon book (₹50L):**

| Sleeve | ₹ | YTM | ModD | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10y G-Sec ladder (direct/TMF) | 15L | 7.1% | 6.5 | spine, sovereign carry |
| SDL sleeve | 15L | 7.4% | 6.8 | spine + state spread, still sovereign-frame |
| AAA NCD basket (5 names) | 12L | 8.2% | 4.2 | muscle, checklist-clean |
| Liquid fund buffer | 5L | 6.3% | 0.1 | fees-free parking, shock absorber |
| One AA NCD (documented exception) | 3L | 9.6% | 3.5 | spread-hunt, 6% single-name |

Portfolio YTM = (15×7.1 + 15×7.4 + 12×8.2 + 5×6.3 + 3×9.6)/50 = 376.2/50 = **7.524%** → income **₹3,76,200/yr**, beating the ₹3.5L requirement with ₹26,200 of slack. Portfolio ModD = (15×6.5 + 15×6.8 + 12×4.2 + 5×0.1 + 3×3.5)/50 = 260.9/50 = **5.218** — inside the 5–5.5 mandate dial.

### 8.2 Pre-signed stress cards (written BEFORE allocation)
- **Rate card:** parallel +1% ⇒ ΔP ≈ −5.218% = **−₹2,61,000 MTM ≈ 8.3 months of income** (2.61/3.76×12). Convexity pads a little; accrual heals ~₹31,350/month. Verdict pre-agreed with client: hold; income unbroken; new money buys 8.5%+.
- **Credit card:** the AA exception (₹3L) defaults, 60% LGD ⇒ **−₹1,80,000 ≈ 5.7 months of portfolio income** — survivable because the muscle obeyed the 6% rule; the memo explains rent math (the sleeve’s whole spread was priced for exactly this).
Two cards, signed at onboarding: when the storm arrives, nobody improvises.

### 8.3 Shock day — the rate memo
Trigger: RBI surprise +50bp, curve bear-flattens, our far-end effectively +1% over the quarter. Book marks −₹2.6L (within the pre-signed −5.2%±). The memo: (1) WHAT happened (see-saw, FI2); (2) what did NOT happen (no default, income ₹31k/month intact); (3) what IMPROVED (reinvestment yield +100bp — every maturing flow now compounds richer; FI2’s mirror); (4) action — hold, redeploy buffer half at the new yields ONLY if horizon math still binds (it does); (5) next stress signposts. A good rate memo converts panic into arithmetic the client already autographed.

### 8.4 Default day — the credit memo
The AA NCD misses a coupon; agencies cut to C; recovery process begins at estimated 60% LGD. The memo: (1) incident + expected loss ₹1.8L (pre-computed on the stress card — no surprise was possible); (2) forensics replay (what the FI6 checklist flagged at entry, what evolved — honest desks audit themselves first); (3) book impact: income dips to ~₹3.47L/yr — mandate breach acknowledged, repair plan: redirect 6 months of buffer + maturing rung into a checklist-clean AAA at the NEW wider spreads (+250bp seasons pay the disciplined); (4) rule reinforcement: the 6% exception cap is WHY this is a memo and not a resignation letter.

### 8.5 The interview forge — defending the desk
Expect: “Why SDLs in a ‘conservative’ book?” (state spread with sovereign-frame history; +30bp × 15L = ₹4,500/yr of free-ish carry matched to the same dial) • “Why ANY AA name?” (priced exception with pre-signed loss card; risk is bought openly or it sneaks in) • “Client demands ≤ 0% NAV drawdown — deliver?” (no honest product offers it above ~6.3%; sell the T-bill/liquid stack and disclose the income gap — never fake the dial) • “Duration 5.2 with rates to rise?” (mandate horizon 6y+ matched; the rise was stress-carded; reducing ModD to 2 costs ≈ ₹51k/yr of income (short stack ~6.5% vs 7.524% on ₹50L) — show both ledgers). Forge rule: every answer contains a number, a precedent, and the client-visible trade-off.
