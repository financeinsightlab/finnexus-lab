# 🎯 BF6 · CAPSTONE — The Behavioral Audit (Twelve Trades on the Couch)
> Five modules of firmware study meet their final exam: a real-looking investor ledger, bias-by-bias, with receipts. Phase 1: autopsy twelve trades — name every bias, price its tuition. Phase 2: stage a bubble to its anatomy. Phase 3: forge your one-page Investment Policy Statement — the constitution future-you can't lobby. Phase 4: the interview forge, where 'how do you beat yourself?' finally has a fluent answer.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Audit a 12-trade ledger: tag the bias(s) per trade, price the extracted tuition, grade process vs outcome separately (BF5 doctrine).
- Map a lived mania to the five stages with exit-opportunity timestamps.
- Draft a personal **IPS**: goals, bands, gates, caps, cooling, journals, review cadence — one page, zero adjectives.
- Deliver the five classic behavioral-finance interviews at deal-flow pace.
- Graduate: carry the armor into Courses 4 (Derivatives), 9 (Portfolio Mgmt), 11 (TVM), 12 (Wealth) where stakes compound.

## 📘 Concepts (the four phases)

### Phase 1 · The ledger autopsy — twelve trades on the couch
Ledger of 'ProfitPrem', 26, Delhi, first salaried year, ₹6L churned:
| # | Trade | Verdict | Bias (tuition) |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Bought hot IPO on listing pop +38% same hour | −11% in a week | availability+herding (−₹6.6k) |
| T2 | sold a quality compounder at +9% 'because profit' | missed +64% next year | concave-gains/reflex bank-it (opportunity invoice) |
| T3 | averaged down PSU-loser 3× from −20% to −55% | still holding | sunk cost+representativeness |
| T4 | F&O weekly expiry 'small fun' runs | −₹1.2L in 5 months | house money+overconfidence (SEBI's exact statistic) |
| T5 | paused 14-month SIP 'markets too high' | missed the run he feared | market-timing mood, stage-3 self-herding |
| T6 | bought tip from group-admin 'he was right twice' | −24% | cascade+authority, zero G2/G3 |
| T7 | profited +40% on an SME pop, rode it to −52% | round trip | house money → disposition, no G6 |
| T8 | panic-sold quality fund at −19% market-wide | locked the bottom | loss aversion + availability avalanche |
| T9 | bonus ₹1.5L → 'vacation + gadgets' in week one | stealth spend | mental-account exemption jar |
| T10 | doubled emergency fund into a 12% FD 'safe' | broke it for T4 margin | mental-account hypocrisy |
| T11 | wrote a journal once: thesis 'boom coming bro' | unusable hindsight prop | process theater (no numbers) |
| T12 | winners: 2 disciplined index SIPs untouched | +16% quiet | the boring armor working |
**Tuition ledger ≈ ₹1.9L in a churned ₹6L year (~32%)** — nearly all of it already named in BF2–BF5; none of it 'bad luck.' The grading line that changes lives: T2 was a D-decision with an A-outcome-till-then (luck's teaser); T12 was an A-decision with whatever-outcome (process). Pay the A's, invoice the D's.

### Phase 2 · Bubble staging drill (generic template)
Take any mania: write the five stages with (a) the displacement technology, (b) the media-mutation phrase that marked euphoria, (c) the scheduled stage-4 trigger (lock-in expiry, rate turn, scandal), (d) the price at stage-5 where YOUR filters (CFO/PAT≥1, ND/EBITDA≤1, insider receipts, BF4.5 kit) would have bought quality survivors cheap. The exercise's spine: **exits are found in stage-2 and stage-3 with rules, not in stage-4 with judgment.**

### Phase 3 · The Investment Policy Statement (one page, forever-ish)
Skeletal IPS (fill YOUR numbers):
1. Goals with dates (house 20xx ₹X; retirement ₹Y @ 60) → required-return math (Course 11).
2. Asset bands: equity 60±10 / debt 35±10 / gold 5±5 — rebalance H1 annually or ±band breach.
3. Gates: BF5's 7-gate card in full; any 2 blank = no trade. Cooling: 24/48h/30-day as drilled.
4. Caps: stock 5%, sector 20%, explore sleeve 5% separate, leverage ≤1.2×, F&O ↔ journal+48h.
5. Journal + review: every decision 2-min entry; monthly decision-grade; annual IPS-amend (rules change only annually, in writing, with a 30-day cool).
6. Kill-switches: listed per sleeve (CFO/PAT<1×2y; insider-pledge; stage-3 barista trigger).
This page beats every 'smart friend' you'll ever meet.

### Phase 4 · The interview forge (rapid-fire bank)
- **'Efficient markets?'** → three forms, anomalies as behavioral scars, exploitation-tax line (BF4 ex.7).
- **'Why do investors sell winners?'** → reference-bound value curve + reflection effect + bank-it reflex; cure: fresh-eyes protocol (BF3).
- **'Your biggest bias and its invoice?'** → honest named bias + rupee tuition + the SPECIFIC gate built against it (self-awareness with receipts is the answer they can't coach).
- **'9 of 10 F&O losers — why?'** → overconfidence triad + churn costs + firmware-hostile arena (SEBI receipts: 9/10, ~₹1.1L avg).
- **'How do you beat yourself?'** → environment > willpower; gate card, cooling, caps, journal, calibration — the five-piece armor with your calibrated hit-rate quote.
