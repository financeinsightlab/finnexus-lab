# BE4 · Talking About Today, Yesterday & Tomorrow: Simple Tense Time-Travel

> *English carries time inside verbs. Three switches — present, past, future — and 90% of daily English is yours. We build each switch with its helper verbs (do/does/did/will), then time-travel a whole diary: habits today, story yesterday, plans tomorrow.*

## 🎯 Objectives
Present simple (habits/facts) + the he/she/it -s rule · present continuous (now!) · past simple (-ed + top-30 irregulars) · future will/going to · questions & negatives per tense · time-words as tense signals · the yesterday-diary + tomorrow-plan project.

---

## 📘 4.1 Present simple — habits, facts & routines

**Form**: subject + verb (I/we/you/they) · subject + verb-**s** (he/she/it!). Negatives: do/does + not + verb (bare). Questions: Do/Does + subject + verb?

```
I work in a shop. / We live in Delhi. / They play cricket on Sundays.
She works in an office. / He goes by bus. / The shop opens at nine.
I do not (don't) eat meat. / She does not (doesn't) like rain.
Do you speak English? / Does he work here? / When does the train leave?
```

**The -S RULE**: ONE subject=he/she/it OR one singular thing ⇒ verb gets -s: "My father drives." "The laptop costs ₹40,000." ⚠️ In ?/− forms the -s jumps into does: ❌ "Does he goes?" ✓ "Does he **go**?" (one -s is enough for the team!)

Signals: always/usually/often/sometimes/never; every day/week/Monday; in the morning; twice a week.
Facts & truths also live here: "The sun rises in the east. Water boils at 100°C. ShopKart sells electronics."

## 📘 4.2 Present continuous — happening NOW (or near plan)

**Form**: am/is/are + verb-**ing**. Negatives: am not / isn't / aren't + -ing. Questions: invert am/is/are.

```
I am writing a letter (now!). She is cooking dinner. We are learning English.
They are watching a movie. It is raining.
What are you doing? — I am reading. / Why is he running? — His bus is leaving ⚡
```

Uses: (a) action right now, (b) these days/temporary ("I am reading a nice book lately."), (c) arranged near future ("We are meeting the teacher tomorrow at five." — diary-locked!).

⚠️ STATIVE verbs hate -ing for states: know, like, love, want, need, have (own), believe, understand. ❌ "I am knowing him." ✓ "I **know** him." (BUT action 'have' is fine: "I am having lunch." = eating!)

## 📘 4.3 Past simple — the story-time machine

**Regular**: verb+**ed** (work→worked, live→lived, stop→stopped, study→studied). **Irregular core-30** (memorize as music 🎵 — chant daily):

```
go→went, come→came, do→did, have→had, be→was/were, eat→ate, drink→drank,
see→saw, take→took, give→gave, get→got, buy→bought, sell→sold,
write→wrote, speak→spoke, tell→told, say→said, make→made, find→found,
think→thought, know→knew, meet→met, teach→taught, bring→brought,
leave→left, feel→felt, run→ran, sleep→slept, pay→paid, cost→cost
```

Negatives/questions use **did**: ❌ "Did you went?" ✓ "Did you **go**?" (did carries the past!) / "I **didn't see** him."

```
Yesterday I woke up late. I had tea and left at nine. The bus was crowded.
I finished work at six. We bought vegetables on the way. It rained at night.
```

Signals: yesterday, last night/week/year, two days ago, in 1999, when I was a child, this morning (finished part!). For completed past action with time stamp = ALWAYS simple past (perfect tenses wait for the Grammar course — park them).

## 📘 4.4 Future: will & going to — plans vs predictions

**will + verb**: instant decisions, promises, predictions (opinion-style). **going to + verb**: fixed plans, visible evidence.

```
I'm thirsty — I'll drink water. (decision NOW)
I will help you tomorrow, promise. (promise)
I think ShopKart will grow next year. (opinion prediction)
We are going to visit Jaipur next month. ✈️ (booked plan)
Look at those clouds — it is going to rain. (evidence!)
```

Shall: polite/offers with I/we → "Shall we go?" / "Shall I open the window?" Negative: won't (=will not). Question: "Will you come?" / "What will we do?"

## 📘 4.5 Time words = tense traffic lights 🚦

| Then → choose | Words |
|---|---|
| Present simple | always, usually, every day, on Mondays, twice a week |
| Continuous | now, right now, at the moment, today (temporary), these days |
| Past simple | yesterday, last week, ago, in 1999, when I was young |
| Future | tomorrow, next week/year, soon, later, in two days |

Read the signal FIRST, then pick the switch — this one habit kills 80% of tense errors.

## 📘 4.6 Mixed error clinic 🏥

1. ❌ Yesterday I go to market. → ✓ I **went** (yesterday = past signal!)
2. ❌ He does not went. → ✓ He did not **go**.
3. ❌ She is go to school now. → ✓ She **is going** (continuous=en-ing) OR She **goes** at nine (routine).
4. ❌ I will going tomorrow. → ✓ I **will go** / I **am going** to go (pick ONE future).
5. ❌ He don't like tea. → ✓ He **doesn't** like tea. (he/she/it = does!)
6. ❌ Tomorrow I am going to office at 6 AM for regular work. → ✓ routine → "I go to office at six" (plans stay plans!)

## 🧪 LAB BE4 — The time-machine project (50 min)

1. **Habit card (present simple)**: 6 sentences about your family routines — include 2 he/she/it -s sentences + 1 question (Does…?) + 1 negative (doesn't).
2. **Right now! (continuous)**: look around — describe 5 ongoing actions ("Mom is cooking. A child is crying outside.") — practice stative fixes: turn "I am knowing" into "I know".
3. **The yesterday story (past simple)**: 8-line diary with at least 6 irregular verbs from the chant (woke, had, went, bought, ate, saw, met…). Circle -ed verbs vs irregulars.
4. **Tomorrow's plan (going to/will)**: 4 going-to plans + 2 will promises/decisions + 1 evidence prediction ("It is going to ___ because ___").
5. **Error surgery**: fix all 6 from §4.6 in writing, then read your diary+plan aloud 3× (recording #2 on phone 🎤 — compare with BE3's by BA — wow-moment guaranteed).

## ❓ Quiz
1. Who gets the -s in present simple, and what happens to it under does/doesn't?
2. "Did you went?" — the one-word verdict and the rule?
3. will vs going to — give the decision/plan distinction in one line?
4. What do words like "yesterday", "ago", "usually" force your brain to do BEFORE conjugating?

### ✅ Answers
1. He/she/it & singular subjects; with does the -s MOVES into "does" — "Does she go?" never "does goes".
2. Wrong — "**did** already carries the past; the main verb goes back to base: Did you GO?"
3. will = decisions/promises/opinion-predictions; going to = fixed plans & evidence-based predictions.
4. Read the time signal then choose the tense — signals first, verbs second. Signal-first thinking = the error-killer habit.
