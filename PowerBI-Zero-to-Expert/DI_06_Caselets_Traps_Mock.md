## 📘 Concepts

### 6.1 The caselet law: tabulate FIRST (never work inside prose)

**Standard caselet:** *\"ShopKart's FY26-Q1 saw revenue grow over Q4-FY25. Online contributed 24% of Q1 sales. Karol Bagh's Q1 was one-sixth of the quarter. Rohini and Saket together made one-fourth. Lajpat Nagar and Dwarka split the remainder in a 6:5 ratio. Total Q1 revenue was ₹89 lakh.\"*

Amateur: re-reads 5 times, panics. Pro: **builds the missing table in 30 seconds:**

```
Total = 89
Online = 24% × 89 = 21.4
KB     = 89 ÷ 6 = 14.83
Rohini + Saket = 89 ÷ 4 = 22.25
Remainder (LN + Dwarka) = 89 − 21.4 − 14.83 − 22.25 = 30.52 … check ratio split:
LN = 30.52 × 6/11 = 16.65 · Dwa = 30.52 × 5/11 = 13.87
```

Now every sub-question (\"LN as % of Online?\" \"KB vs Rohini+Saket share gap?\") is plain DI2 table math on YOUR table. **The caselet never tests new math — it tests whether you convert words to grid before calculating.** Fractions given as \"one-sixth\", \"24%\", \"6:5\" are the skeleton; the total is the anchor; everything hangs off them.

### 6.2 Mixed sets: each figure owns ONE job

*Bar chart: quarter totals 58/65/76/81. Line overlay: Online share % per quarter (20.7, 21.5, 23.7, 27.2). Table below: store ranks per quarter.*

Rules of engagement:
- **One figure = one role:** totals come from the bars, shares from the line, ranks from the table. Never estimate a share from a bar.
- **Cross-format questions are two-step by design:** \"Online's Q2 ₹?\" = bar total × line share = 65 × 21.5% = 14.0 ✔ (matches the table's 14 — trust but verify).
- **The stitched-inconsistency trap:** if charts disagree by rounding (line says 23.7%, table implies 23.68%), take the **more precise table** for finals, the chart for estimates.

### 6.3 The five DI traps (vaccine list — say the names)

1. **Unit mismatch** — lakh vs thousand vs crore; also \"in 000s\" headers. *Vaccine: one-unit line before computing (DI1).*
2. **Percentage vs percentage POINTS** — \"share rose from 20.7% to 27.2%\" = +6.5 POINTS, which is +31.4% relative. *Vaccine: when both quantities are %, whisper \"points or percent?\"*
3. **Base confusion** — %-change divides by the FIRST/first-mentioned value; \"highest growth\" ≠ \"highest value\". *Vaccine: \"of WHAT?\" + underline most-vs-fastest (DI2/DI3).*
4. **Axis lies & scale breaks** — non-zero baselines, broken axes. *Vaccine: 3-second audit → numbers-only mode (DI3).*
5. **Rounding cascades** — rounding %: 23.57% × 280 = 65.996 ≈ 66 ✔ but rounding INPUT twice can flip tight options. *Vaccine: round once, at the END (DI1 approximation law).*

### 6.4 Set triage: the full-section pace plan

```
DIFFICULTY / TIME RANKING (per set, typical banking-SSC):
   Table (cleanest) → Bar → Pie → Line → Caselet/Mixed (greediest)
PACING:  4 sets × 5 Q in ~25 min → ~75 s/Q budget; DI1's 4-step routine
Bank first: clean Table & Bar sets at Q1–10; Caselets LAST (they pay the same!)
Skip rule: any single Q > 120 s → mark, move, return only with surplus time.
```

## 🏟️ THE DI MOCK ARENA — Paper DI-01 (10 Qs · 8 min · −0.25 self-marking)

*Use ALL course data: FY25 store table (DI2) + quarters 58/65/76/81 + Online monthly (DI4) + both pies (DI5) + caselet 6.1. Timer on BEFORE Q1.*

**Q1.** Difference between FY-quarter totals Q4 and Q1 (₹L).
(a) 19 (b) 21 (c) 23 (d) 25

**Q2.** Highest QoQ %-growth among FY25 quarters?
(a) Q1→Q2: 12.1% (b) Q2→Q3: 16.9% (c) Q3→Q4: 6.2% (d) All equal

**Q3.** Online's FY25 share of total revenue:
(a) 21.4% (b) 23.6% (c) 25.0% (d) 27.2%

**Q4.** If FY26-Q1 is 10% above FY25-Q4, FY26-Q1 total (₹L) =
(a) 89.1 (b) 81.0 (c) 90.0 (d) 72.9

**Q5.** Karol Bagh's slice in the FY25 store pie measures…
(a) 54° (b) 59.1° (c) 69.4° (d) 84.9°

**Q6.** FY25 net profit at 2.4% margin on ₹280L is…
(a) ₹6.72L (b) ₹4.72L (c) ₹8.6L (d) ₹2.4L

**Q7.** Steepest month-to-month %-jump on the Online line:
(a) Nov→Dec 18.2% (b) Sep→Oct 20% (c) Jan→Feb 7.1% (d) Jun→Jul 12.5%

**Q8.** Rohini's FY sales as a % of Dwarka's:
(a) 90.5% (b) 100% (c) 110.5% (d) 113.6%

**Q9.** (Unit trap) The FY25 table is in ₹ lakh. Karol Bagh's FY sales written in FULL ₹:
(a) ₹5,40,000 (b) ₹54,00,000 (c) ₹5.4 crore (d) ₹54,000

**Q10.** GST at 18% on Fresh Farms invoice FF-221 of ₹47,250; total billing =
(a) ₹55,755 (b) ₹50,032.5 (c) ₹8,505 (d) ₹56,025

### ✅ Mock Arena Key & Analysis

1. **(c) 23** — 81 − 58. (Warm-up, 15 s.)
2. **(b)** — 11/65 = 16.9% max (12.1%, 16.9%, 6.2%). (% race, base respected.)
3. **(b)** — 66/280 = 23.57 ≈ 23.6%. (Totals-first skeleton!)
4. **(a)** — 81 × 1.1 = 89.1. (Anchor method: 81 + 8.1.)
5. **(c)** — 19.29% × 3.6 = 69.4°. (Tri-conversion; 54° is Rohini's bait.)
6. **(a)** — 2.4% × 280 = 6.72L — the GL's net ₹6.7L story ✔.
7. **(b)** — 1.0/5 = 20% > 1.0/5.5 = 18.2% (small-base law on slopes).
8. **(c)** — 42/38 = 110.5%. ('Of'-denominator = Dwarka.)
9. **(b)** — ₹54 lakh = 54 × 100,000 = ₹54,00,000. (a) and (d) are the thousand-trap; (c) is 10× over.
10. **(a)** — ₹47,250 + ₹8,505 = ₹55,755. Option (c) is the GST-ONLY bait (question asked TOTAL). Read what is asked: tax, or taxed-total?

**Score:** /10 raw, −0.25/wrong. 8+ = exam-ready. Under 6? → journal each miss by trap name (unit? base? pp?) and re-run in 48 h.

## ❓ Quiz

**Q1.** The first move on ANY caselet is:
- (a) Answer the easiest sub-question from the prose directly
- (b) **TABULATE — convert the paragraph's fractions/ratios/percents into a value table using the given TOTAL as anchor**; then it's plain table DI
- (c) Draw a pie chart of it
- (d) Skip — caselets are trap sets

**Q2.** \"Online's share rose from 20.7% to 27.2%.\" The increase stated CORRECTLY is:
- (a) 6.5%
- (b) **6.5 percentage POINTS — which is +31.4% in relative terms (6.5 ÷ 20.7)**; percent-vs-points is the vaccine-2 whisper
- (c) 27.2%
- (d) 20.7%

**Q3.** In a full DI section, the correct pacing order is:
- (a) Caselets first — get them done
- (b) **Clean tables & bars FIRST, pies/lines next, caselets & mixed sets LAST** — same marks per question, so buy the cheap ones first; skip-rule at 120 s
- (c) Strict paper order
- (d) Whatever looks colorful

### ✅ Answers

1. **(b)** — the tabulate-first law. Caselets hide zero new math; they test conversion discipline. Words → grid → calculator-fast answers.
2. **(b)** — both statements can be true ONLY when named correctly: +6.5 points AND +31.4% relative. Exam options weaponize the un-named version.
3. **(b)** — set triage: cheap marks first, greedy formats last, skip-rule written down. DI sections are won by order, not heroics.
