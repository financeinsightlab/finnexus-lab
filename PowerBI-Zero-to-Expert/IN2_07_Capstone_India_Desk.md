## 📘 Concepts

### 7.1 The sequence is the strategy
Priya’s failure modes in order of lethality: (1) no emergency cash → any shock fires a panic-sell at the bottom; (2) no health/term cover → one hospital bill Liquidates the future; (3) tips and IPO FOMO → tuition in a leveraged costume. So the desk’s order of operations is fixed by threat, not by excitement: **protection** (term plan ~₹1Cr for dependents-to-be, health ₹10L floater; parents’ cover verified) → **liquidity** (₹1.5L emergency stack: ₹50k savings sweep + ₹1L liquid fund/FD ladder — target: reachable in 24–72h, yield irrelevant) → **investing** (the SIP engine) → **speculation** (stock sleeve, ≤ caps, only after the engine runs 3 months untouched). Inverted sequences are why smart people exit markets poorer — the pyramid stands or the pyramid falls; there is no third option.

### 7.2 Accounts & the paper spine
KYC once (Aadhaar-based, CKYC registry): opens the door to brokers, AMCs, depositories. Desk checklist: demat+trading (discount broker; nominee ADDED DAY ONE — an unnominated account is a probate gift to lawyers), MF via direct route (AMC sites/MFU), RBI Retail Direct (FI5 govvie parking when the emergency stack outgrows ₹3L), CAS email ON (the monthly x-ray of every folio), and one master spreadsheet: folio, platform, nominee, amount — the documentPriya’s family finds if Tuesday goes wrong. Boring? The boring layer is the difference between an investor and a deceased investor’s court case.

### 7.3 The ₹15k engine — installed
₹60k take − ₹25k expenses − ₹10k emergency-build (until month 10) − ₹10k buffer/other = **₹15k investable** steady-state (post month-10 the ₹10k joins, taking it to ₹25k — but we install conservatively). Split: **₹10,500 index core** (NIFTY 50 + Next-50 index fund, direct plan — IN2 3’s obedience machine at 0.1–0.3% TER) · **₹3,750 active satellite** (a flexicap with a decade of process evidence — the one place an opinion is paid for) · **₹750 gold/debt sleeve** (multi-asset or gilt TMF — the hedge that makes −30% tapes survivable). Settings: step-up +10% every April (IN2 6’s compounding lever), auto-debit dated 2 days after salary, **no SIP-pause button tolerated** (BF6 weld — the pause is where retail returns go to die). 20-year projection at 12%: corpus ≈ ₹1.5Cr+ on the ₹15k version — the number that converts the tips group’s dopamine into embarrassment.

### 7.4 First trade + the audit drill
Month 4, engine running, sleeve funded ₹1.5L: Priya buys her first FA7-verdict stock — **100 @ ₹1,500**. Contract note lands: gross ₹1,50,000 + ₹202.47 charges (STT 150, brokerage 20, txn 5.25, SEBI 0.15, GST 4.57, stamp 22.50) = **₹1,50,202.47**, effective ₹1,502.02/share. Desk ritual: (1) verify each line against IN2 5’s rate card (divergence = call the broker, politely, in writing — machines err in their own favor more than chance allows); (2) journal the thesis in 5 lines BEFORE the first red day (entry logic, target horizon, kill-switch conditions, position size reasoning, expected tax head) — the journal is the only antidote to post-hoc storytelling; (3) set the review calendar (quarterly thesis-check, NOT daily price-check). The trade is small; the PIPELINE it rehearses is the career.

### 7.5 The purge + the signed IPS
**Purge run (one evening):** leave/mute every tips and pump group (IN2 6’s tells — guaranteed, urgent, admin-bought microcaps); verify any advisor on SEBI’s registry (result: cousin’s “advisor friend” = unregistered, scripted refusal deployed); unsubscribe “research” spam; install the freeze: no new product without a 48-hour cooling note (BF3’s cooling periods, personal edition). **IPS one-pager, signed:** goal ladder (emergency ✓ → house in 7y → retirement) · allocation (70/25/5 engine + sleeves) · caps (single stock ≤5% of portfolio, F&O = zero, IPO pops ≤ ₹15k/yr lottery budget with written exit) · behavior clauses (no pause, 48h cooling, quarterly reviews only, tax-harvest every March within ₹1.25L) · audit trail (CAS + journal + ITR filed on time, losses banked). Priya signs it; the market can now do its worst — the system already answered.
