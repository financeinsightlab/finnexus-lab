## 📘 Concepts

### 6.1 ☠️ The 5 deadliest traps (catalogue & vaccines)

**Trap 1 — Average of averages ≠ average.**
"Class A avg 60, Class B avg 80 → combined 70?" Only if sizes match! **Vaccine:** shouting "sizes?" before every combined-average question; weighted merge otherwise (AV3/AV4).

**Trap 2 — Not-out divisor (cricket twist).**
300 runs / 10 innings with 2 not-outs → 37.5, not 30. **Vaccine:** read for "not out" in every cricket line; average over dismissals.

**Trap 3 — Speed: distance vs time weights.**
60/40 round trip = 48, not 50. **Vaccine:** mantra "distance equal → harmonic; time equal → simple; else total ÷ total."

**Trap 4 — Count-off-by-one (n = last − first + 1) & who-existed-then.**
21..40 has 20 terms; the newborn isn't in the birth-cohort. **Vaccine:** the +1 chant; underlining "at the time of birth" to fix the divisor.

**Trap 5 — Golden property overreach.**
"Some numbers increase by 5 → average rises by 5?" Only if EVERY value rose AND n stayed put. One join/leave → engine. **Vaccine:** two-question gate — "all values changed?" + "n unchanged?" — before any property.

### 6.2 🎯 The 4 elimination tactics (solve before solving)

MCQs forgive you for never computing, if you can legally rule out:

| Tactic | How | Instant demo |
|---|---|---|
| **Range-band** | Combined/weighted average must lie between the group averages, closer to the bigger group | A(20) avg 40 + B(10) avg 70: options 35, 45, **50**, 65, 75 → only 45–65 legal-ish; nearer 40's weight → ✓ instantly |
| **Sense-sign** | Direction must make sense: above-average joiner RAISES avg | Joiner above avg but option shows a fall → delete it, decide among the rest |
| **Unit-check** | Answer must match unit + magnitude (marks ≠ kg ≠ ₹) | Options in different magnitudes often expose the setter's intended unit path |
| **Back-plug** | Put the middle option INTO the totals engine — check last | Cheaper than algebra: test option C first, move up/down by sign error |

Exams reward the full squad: formula-first candidates score 70%; formula+elimination candidates score 100% *faster*.

### 6.3 🛡️ The 3 verification rituals (never lose a mark you earned)

1. **Deviation-zero:** for any raw-data average, deviations from your answer must sum to 0 (AV1's gift).
2. **Engine-recheck:** for group-change, totals BEFORE → event → AFTER must reconcile exactly.
3. **Option-consistency:** your value must be legal for every condition in the question — re-read the last line for "average must remain ≤…", "exactly", "at most".

Ritual time budget: 5–10 seconds each. Cheaper insurance than re-solving whole questions.

### 6.4 ⏱️ Time economics (how the pros pace it)

- **Target speeds:** direct-trick questions ≤ 45 s · engine questions ≤ 90 s · age/cricket classics ≤ 60 s · mock max = 15 min/10 Qs.
- **Two-pass rule:** solve all ≤60-s questions first pass; mark long engines for pass 2. Never sink 3 minutes as the 4-minute questions queue behind.
- **The diary habit (non-negotiable):** log every wrong answer in a 3-column journal: `question code · trap-name (6.1 list) · the fix`. After 2 mocks, your personal trap profile argues with nobody.

### 6.5 🏟️ THE MOCK ARENA (10 Qs / 15 min / closed notes)

Set a timer. Answers hidden below in Selected answers. Scoring bands: 9–10 = placement-ready · 7–8 = strong · 5–6 = review Modules 3–4 · <5 = revisit from AV2 (fight!).

1. The average of 6 numbers is 20. If one number, 30, is removed, the average of the rest is…?
2. Average of the first 50 natural numbers.
3. Class of 25, avg 64. Teacher joins → avg 65. Teacher's marks?
4. The average weight of 8 people rises by 2 kg when a 60 kg person is replaced. New person's weight?
5. Two sections: 20 students avg 55, 30 students avg 70. Combined average, one decimal.
6. Batsman: avg 28 after 14 innings (incl. 2 not outs = CAREFUL). Runs needed in innings 15–16 (both dismissals) to reach career avg 32 over the same dismissals logic… treat totals normally: current total = 28×14 = 392; target = 32×16 = 512 → needed = 120. (this one GIVES the engine steps — electricity question: is average "aggregate ÷ innings" valid here, or if you take the not-out route, how do answer options change? THINK then pick your method and note your reasoning! 😉) — reframe properly: how many runs in 2 innings if every innings counts? → **120**. If dismissals-only (2 NO in first 14), what's the batsman's *true* current batting average?
7. Round trip 90 km/h out, 60 km/h back. Average speed?
8. Computed avg of 20 students = 68; one entry 86 was misread as 56. Correct average?
9. Sum of 7 consecutive numbers = 161. Middle term?
10. Officer/clerk salaries: 4 officers avg ₹90k, 21 clerks avg ₹40k. True company average?

## ❓ Quiz

1. The vaccine for "average of averages" (Trap 1) begins with the question…
2. Back-plug works fastest when you start from the…
3. In your error journal, the middle column should be the trap's…

### ✅ Answers

1. **"Are the sizes equal?"** — if not, weighted merge; saying the vaccine aloud in the exam hall (in your head!) stops the reflex.
2. **Middle option (usually C)** — its failure direction tells you whether the answer is higher or lower, halving the remaining work.
3. **Name** (from §6.1's catalogue). Naming the trap rewires instinct: next time the pattern appears, the vaccine fires before the trap.
