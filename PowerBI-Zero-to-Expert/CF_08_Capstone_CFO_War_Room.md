## 🎯 Objectives

## 📘 Concepts (the four phases)

### Phase 1 · Hurdle sprint — statements to 12.6% without notes
Ke = 7 + 1.1×6 = 13.6% · Kd = 2.3/19 = 12.1% → ×(1−0.2517) = 9.1% · weights 76.9/23.1 (book, private firm — honest here). WACC = 0.769×13.6 + 0.231×9.1 = **12.6%**. Interview garnish: "private firm ⇒ book weights and an estimated beta from listed retail comps, unlevered+relevered; annual refresh, not market-mood refresh."

### Phase 2 · Project triage (all cash flows ₹L, post-tax FCFF-style)
```text
A · Dark store     capex 40, inflows 12 × 5y, salvage ~0
   NPV@12.6% = 12 × 3.552 − 40 = **+2.6**   IRR ≈ **15.2%**  ⇒ ACCEPT (thin, watch CCC)
B · Billboard blitz spend 8 for +0.9L/yr EBIT — year-one yield 0.9/8 = 11.25% < hurdle ⇒ REJECT (spread −1.35pp)
C · Delivery fleet capex 15, FCFF ≈ 3.6 × 5y + salvage 2: NPV ≈ −0.9 borderline ⇒ STRATEGIC REVIEW
```
**B's funeral line:** an 11% return funded with 9.1% debt looks accretive — until renewal, distress neighborhoods, and the equity-paid-for risk arrive; spread −1.6pp = destroy ₹0.13/₹/yr in EVA terms.
**C's honest frame:** fleet buys delivery-speed (experience moat) not shown in FCFF; price the option: incremental margin from 30-min delivery promise × adoption − leakage; if the option can't be sized at ≥ ₹0.9L/yr, defer C — *strategic* is a budget line, not a spell.

### Phase 3 · Funding stack with covenant math
Pecking order: deploy **cash ₹12L** (idle at ~6% FD while projects pay >12.6%) + **₹28L term loan @ 11%** for A (asset-backed tenor match — not the OD!). Post-deal guardrails: interest 2.3 + 3.1 = 5.4; EBIT base 20 + project's EBIT ~5 ⇒ coverage = 25/5.4 ≈ **4.6×** ≥ 4× floor ✓; D/E = (19+28)/(63.4) = **0.74** ≤ 1.0 covenant ✓. **Stress rehearsal (CF4's gears):** a −10% sales shock swings EBIT by −40.6% (DOL 4.06): trough EBIT = 25 × 0.594 = 14.9 ⇒ coverage = 14.9/5.4 = **2.76×** — above the 2× emergency floor but inside the dividend-lock zone (trigger < 3×). Publish that lock *before* signing: boards respect rehearsed pain and punish improvised pain.

### Phase 4 · Board letter + the forge
**Board letter skeleton (yours must read like this):** (i) recommendation + one-line spread logic; (ii) funding stack + covenant posture with stress numbers; (iii) what we declined and why (B's ghost will reappear yearly — kill it loudly); (iv) watch list: CCC ≤ 76 days, coverage quarterly ≥ 4×, dark-store GMROI ≥ 2.2 by year 2 or exit-pivot.
**Rapid-fire bank (train until bored):**
- *WACC +1pp?* → TV compresses ~11–13%, A's NPV ₹2.6L → ~−1.5L: the whole go-decision hangs on input hygiene — which is why Phase 1 deserves its own reviewer.
- *Why not OD for fit-outs?* → 8-year asset vs recallable-any-day money: renewal roulette; rate saves 1% and buys refinancing terror (AC4's mismatch doctrine).
- *"EPS accretive" — so?* → accretion arithmetic ≠ value; show me spread over WACC and the CCC it drags (CF1's guillotine).
- *Dividend during expansion?* → residual doctrine: pause the band only with breach-conditions published in advance (CF5), or payout discipline dies as 'temporary' forever.
- *Biggest WACC bluff in decks?* → single hurdle for property + tech + retail (CF2's felony #1).

## 🧪 LAB — War-room drills (10 min)
1. NPV solo: capex 25, FCFF 6.5 × 5y, WACC 12.6%. Verdict + the margin comment.
2. IRR shortcut sanity: for project A, explain *why* 12 × 3.552 pricing means IRR sits above 12.6% without computing it.
3. Covenant letter: write the three numbers (coverage min, D/E max, dividend-lock trigger) you as lender impose on ShopKart's ₹28L loan.
4. Spread autopsy: promoter claims the billboard earns 12.5% ('so close to 12.6%!'). Your two-line kill.
5. Stress check: new coverage floor test at EBIT trough −20% using DCL 4.59 — numbers.

**🔑 Lab answers:** (1) 6.5 × 3.552 = 23.09 − 25 = **−1.9 ⇒ REJECT**; margin comment: 'NPV margin −7.6% of capex — the "close" feeling is the trap; thin negatives compound' (2) because ₹2.6L of PV surplus exists AT 12.6%, a higher rate that zeroes it exists — that's IRR: no arithmetic needed, just the definition (3) min coverage 4×, max D/E 1.0 including this loan, dividend lock if coverage < 3× — all quarterly-tested (4) '12.5% < 12.6% destroys value by construction; "close" is for horseshoes — show a strategic cross-sell engine or kill it yearly' (5) −20% sales shock (deep-recession drill): EBIT = 25 × (1 − 0.2 × 4.06) = 25 × 0.188 = **4.7** ⇒ coverage = 4.7 / 5.4 = **0.87×** — operations cannot service interest at that depth. The doctrine takeaway: this is exactly why the loan was capped at ₹28L (not the tempting ₹34L), why the dividend-lock publishes in advance, and why the CCC war-plan (AC6 canon: 10 days = ₹0.65L) is stapled to the loan file — debt size is chosen at the trough, not at the ribbon-cutting.

## ❓ Quiz
1. Project A (capex 40, FCFF 12 × 5y, WACC 12.6%) presents NPV +₹2.6L and IRR ≈ 15.2%:
   - (a) reject — margin too thin to matter
   - (b) accept with monitoring — positive spread is positive value; thinness is a WATCH-LIST instruction (CCC creep, ramp pace), not a rejection reason; IRR 250bp above hurdle covers honest forecast error
   - (c) accept and forget
2. The billboard "it earns 12.5%, basically at hurdle" is killed by:
   - (a) billboard ugliness
   - (b) sub-WACC is sub-WACC — negative spread destroys value at any closeness; equity pays 13.6% for its risk and the deck wants 12.5%; "close" romanticism is how CFO desks fill with compounding regrets (EVA ledger remembers the −0.1pp indefinitely)
   - (c) inflation
3. Funding the expansion with the recallable-any-day OD instead of a term loan would:
   - (a) save 1% — do it
   - (b) commit the AC4 mismatch felony: 8-year assets on 8-day money, every renewal a coin-flip THROUGH the trough (lenders flee exactly in recessions); the 1% saved is the charge for carrying refinancing terror all tenure — match tenor, sleep, then compare rates
   - (c) be illegal

### ✅ Answers
1. **(b)** — (a) outsources judgment to a vibe; (c) outsources it to amnesia; (b) is doctrine plus discipline.
2. **(b)** — value is spread arithmetic, not grade-school rounding; sub-hurdle is destruction in a tuxedo.
3. **(b)** — mismatch first, rate second, always in that order; (c) is false, (a) is how the story starts.
