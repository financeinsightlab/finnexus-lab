## 📘 Concepts

### 5.1 Process beats personality — the discipline equation
Desirable-U = f(environment, rules, review), never f(resolution). Three failure modes of resolutions: they operate exactly when System 1 is hottest (BF1), they have no audit trail (hindsight edits them, BF2.5), and they're all stick no architecture (temptation remains on the home screen). **Stack doctrine: every good behavior gets an automatic path; every bad behavior gets friction, delay, and an audit.** You don't rise to your goals; you fall to your systems.

### 5.2 The 7-gate pre-trade checklist (any 2 blank = NO TRADE)
```text
G1 THESIS, dated & written before quote-checking           G5 SIZE within caps (see 5.5)
G2 BASE RATE consulted (what do such bets usually do?)     G6 EXIT pre-planned (kill-switch fact + review date)
G3 DISCONFIRM search completed (the one fact that kills it) G7 VALUATION link: price vs MY band (CF7), not vs anchors
G4 JOURNAL entry inked (5.3 format, dated)
```
Each gate targets a named bias: G2 kills availability+overconfidence, G3 kills confirmation, G4 kills hindsight, G5 kills house-money, G7 kills anchoring. The checklist's superpower is not intelligence — it's that System 1 (BF1) can't negotiate with a laminated card.

### 5.3 The decision journal — your audited twin
Entry format (2 minutes, dated): **Action · thesis in one line · current price vs my range · confidence tag (A/B/C) · expected outcome & horizon · kill-switch fact.** Monthly review ritual (30 min): P&L irrelevant; grade DECISION quality — was reasoning complete vs outcome lucky/unlucky? Calibration ledger: of my A-tags, what % won? (Healthy adults discover even their A's hit ~60%. The day your confidence >= your hit-rate + 0 is the day humility became arithmetic.)

### 5.4 Commitment architecture — jars that work FOR you
- **SIP autopilot:** the purchase happens without a mood vote; rupee-cost averaging (₹5,000 buys 125 units at NAV 40 vs 83 at NAV 60) converts volatility from threat into discount calendar.
- **EPF/PPF/lock-ins:** penalties as FEATURES — friction applied in the right direction.
- **Windfall quarantine (BF3 governor):** 30 days liquid-fund parking + written allocation; jackpot never meets adrenaline.
- **Cooling periods:** buys 24h journals-first; leverage/F&O 48h; position-size changes 7 days. Delays are cheap humiliation insurance: every urge that survives 48 hours has earned an argument at least.

### 5.5 Position-size law — math before courage
Caps at the account level (example frame — tune to your IPS): single stock ≤ 5% of equity book (multi-bagger dreams don't outrank amputation math, BF4.3); sector ≤ 20%; F&O/exploration sleeve ≤ 5% and SEPARATE account (physical friction); leverage ≤ 1.2× gross, zero for F&O-be-ginners (the SEBI 9-in-10 poster applies). **Sizing is the only bias-cure that works even while you're wrong about everything else:** it buys you the right to be wrong repeatedly without dying — which is precisely the skill markets actually pay for.
