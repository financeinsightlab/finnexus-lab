## 📘 Concepts

### 12.0 The file at a glance — tonight's data pack

**Meridian Ltd** (Indian parent, rupees) bought **80% of ForgeTech Pvt Ltd** on 1 April for **₹400cr cash**. Day-0 due diligence: ForgeTech's net assets at book ₹280cr; an unrecorded brand worth +₹40cr at fair value; deferred-tax liability on the uplift at 25%, ₹10cr — net assets at fair value **₹310cr**. Policy file says proportionate NCI. Year-1 ledgers: parent standalone profit ₹100cr (including ₹8cr of dividend income from ForgeTech); ForgeTech profit ₹50cr; ForgeTech declared AND paid a ₹10cr dividend (₹8cr to Meridian, ₹2cr to the minority). The deal stack still runs: extra depreciation on the uplift ₹10cr a year, its DTL release ₹2.5cr a year (DT doctrine, standing file). Workshop items: an intra receivable/payable of ₹20cr; Meridian sold goods to ForgeTech with ₹4cr of profit still sleeping in ForgeTech's closing stock (downstream); Meridian sold a lathe to ForgeTech at a ₹6cr gain with 5 years of life left. The foreign file: ForgeTech's US wing carries a ₹4cr CTA credit at signing day (CS10). The board's question on your desk: a buyer offers **₹420cr for 55 points** of Meridian's 80; the retained 25% would fair-value at ₹140cr; ForgeTech's net assets then stand at ₹525cr; the NCI bay by then holds ₹72cr (the ₹68.5cr you will build tonight plus a stub-year share of profit ₹2.5cr and a ₹1.0cr slice of fresh CTA).

### 12.1 Exhibit 1 — the acquisition-day grid (CS4 doctrine)

First question of the night: what number stands for the Stepmother at birth? Three candidates on the grid. Parent-price parity: the bid implies a whole of ₹500cr (400 ÷ 0.8), so 20% would be ₹100cr — rejected, because the parent's price carries a control premium the minority never paid; feeding it to the NCI inflates both goodwill and the minority bay. Proportionate share of identifiable net assets at fair value: 310 × 20% = **₹62cr** — the policy file's door, Ind AS 103's default. Independently appraised fair value ₹77.5cr — the full-goodwill fork, logged but not taken. Goodwill = consideration + NCI − net assets at FV = 400 + 62 − 310 = **₹152cr**. Fork echo for the standing file: in the ₹77.5cr world, goodwill prints ₹167.5cr (400 + 77.5 − 310) and every future impairment ceremony lands ₹15.5cr heavier on a richer base. Opening elimination: Dr identifiable net assets ₹310cr, Dr goodwill ₹152cr, Cr investment ₹400cr, Cr NCI ₹62cr — 462 = 462, the vanishing investment line of CS3, proven in one sentence.

### 12.2 Exhibit 2 — the elimination workshop (CS6 doctrine), four pairs

**Pair 1, the ₹20cr receivable/payable:** Dr intra payables ₹20cr, Cr intra receivables ₹20cr — profit untouched, the balance sheet honestly smaller; the creature cannot owe itself money. **Pair 2, the downstream UPP ₹4cr:** Dr group profit ₹4cr, Cr inventory ₹4cr — the parent's skin booked the sale; the goods never left the creature; owners bear 100% because the NCI's skin holds only stock, not the profit (CS6: downstream dies entirely on the family that sold; the ₹1-law rhythm releases it when outsiders finally buy). **Pair 3, the dividend:** the parent's ₹8cr of dividend income dies against the intra-family transfer — income cannot be manufactured by moving money between your own pockets — while the ₹2cr leg to the minority is REAL cash leaving the creature: it exits through financing in the group cash flow (CS11) and as a deduction in the NCI walk. **Pair 4, the lathe:** Dr gain on sale ₹6cr, Cr machinery ₹6cr kills tonight's fantasy profit; then the mercy, Dr accumulated depreciation ₹1.2cr, Cr depreciation expense ₹1.2cr — the machine re-anchors to its old ₹35L-a-year world and the group absorbs **−₹4.8cr** net this year. The standing file schedules ₹1.2cr of annual relief for years 2–5: an elimination with no scheduled future is an elimination that will be wrong by year three.

### 12.3 Exhibit 3 — the attribution weave (CS5 doctrine), six moves

**Move 1:** strip the family fiction from the parent: 100 − 8 dividend income = **92.0**. **Move 2:** run the deal stack through the child: 50 − 10 extra depreciation + 2.5 DTL release = **42.5** — every paisa of the acquisition's aftermath flows through the subsidiary's ADJUSTED profit, because that is the profit the NCI co-owns. **Move 3:** land the UPP downstream: owners −4.0, child untouched. **Move 4:** group profit = 92.0 + 42.5 − 4.0 = **₹130.5cr**. **Move 5:** the Stepmother claims 20% × 42.5 = **₹8.5cr** — attribution on adjusted profit, deal stack included. **Move 6:** owners' share = 130.5 − 8.5 = **₹122.0cr**. Close-checks, both mandatory: 122.0 + 8.5 = 130.5 ✓; and the naive-view reconciliation — the street added 100 + 50 = 150, your file shows 130.5, and the entire ₹19.5 gap narrates itself: −8 dividend double-count, −10 deal depreciation, +2.5 deferred-tax mercy, −4.0 sleeping UPP. A weave you cannot reconcile to the naive view is a weave that is wrong.

### 12.4 Exhibit 4 — the NCI walk, two routes, one answer (CS5)

**Route 1, the walk:** open at birth ₹62cr + her share of the child's adjusted profit ₹8.5cr − dividends paid to her ₹2cr = **₹68.5cr**. **Route 2, the basis:** she owns 20% of the child's fair-value-plus-adjusted net assets — 20% × (310 + 42.5 − 10) = 20% × 342.5 = **₹68.5cr** ✓. Two arithmetic roads, one doorstep — exactly the discipline CS5 demanded. The intern's error tonight would be 20% × ₹280cr book = ₹56cr: wrong basis entirely, because her claim was born on FAIR values (the brand, the tax shadow) and grows on ADJUSTED profit; book value died on day-0. Memorize the corrected sentence: NCI equals 20% of ₹342.5cr, never 20% of ₹280cr.

### 12.5 Exhibit 5 — goodwill: the geometry and the surrender (CS7)

₹152cr sits in the group balance sheet unamortized — tested, never ticked away. Tonight's impairment stress test says the ForgeTech CGU's recoverable amount undershoots carrying by ₹40cr. If that lands, the knife splits by attribution: owners 32, NCI 8 (proportionate policy deems the NCI's goodwill slice to be her share — CS7's geometry). The re-walk: 62 + 8.5 − 2 − 8 = **₹60.5cr**, and owners' profit would print 122.0 − 32 = 90.0. Tonight's pack PASSES its test, so the ₹40cr stays a flip-drill rather than a signed number — but the ceremony grammar is rehearsed because goodwill surrenders all at once, in public, and never reverses: a surrendered rupee stays surrendered; only the standing file remembers the storm. Fork echo: in the ₹167.5cr full-goodwill world the same test swings a heavier blade at precisely the moments the group can least afford the theatre — the convention chosen at birth echoes for a decade.

### 12.6 Exhibit 6 — the control-cliff ceremony, priced twice (CS8)

The board's offer on the desk: ₹420cr for 55 points, 25% retained. The ceremony grammar, every term earning its seat: + proceeds ₹420cr, + retained slice re-measured at fair value ₹140cr, + NCI derecognized ₹72cr, + CTA recycled to P&L ₹4cr credit (CS10: the bay empties the day the family tie breaks), − net assets derecognized ₹525cr, − goodwill derecognized ₹152cr. Arithmetic: (420 + 140 + 72 + 4) − (525 + 152) = 636 − 677 = **−₹41cr LOSS**. At ₹480cr the identical ceremony prints **+₹19cr GAIN** (696 − 677). The lesson is the deepest in the course: the ceremony promises TRUTH, not a gain — it re-measures everything you keep, recycles everything OCI stored for you, and lets the buyer's price argue with ₹677cr of derecognized carrying amount. Solve the neutral price: P + 216 − 677 = 0 gives **₹461cr**. Tonight's memo: decline at 420, reopen at 480-plus, never sign below 461 — the consolidation file just saved the chairman ₹60cr of negotiating blindness.

### 12.7 The partner's ten-question cross — 05:30 sharp

**1.** Why ₹62cr and not ₹100cr for the NCI at birth? — Proportionate share of identifiable net assets at FAIR value; the parent's implied ₹500cr whole is control-premium polluted, and the minority never paid a premium. **2.** Where does the ₹8cr dividend income go? — It dies; intra-family transfers are not income to the creature (the ₹2cr minority leg is real and exits via financing). **3.** Why didn't the ₹4cr UPP touch the NCI? — Downstream: the parent's skin booked the profit, the child's skin only holds the stock; owners bear 100%. **4.** Flip the UPP upstream — who bleeds? — The child's skin booked it, so attribution splits the wound: owners ₹3.2cr, NCI ₹0.8cr. **5.** The lathe at year-2? — Dr opening retained earnings ₹4.8cr, Dr accumulated depreciation ₹1.2cr, Cr machinery ₹6.0cr; then the year's relief, Dr accumulated depreciation ₹1.2cr, Cr depreciation expense ₹1.2cr — standing file carries it to year-5. **6.** NCI tonight, no impairment? — 62 + 8.5 − 2 = ₹68.5cr. **7.** And with a ₹40cr goodwill impairment? — NCI −8 → ₹60.5cr; owners −32. **8.** The street's naive ₹150cr versus your ₹130.5cr — what ate ₹19.5cr? — ₹8cr dividend double-count, ₹10cr deal depreciation, ₹4cr sleeping UPP, net of ₹2.5cr of deferred-tax mercy. **9.** Sell 55 of 80 at ₹420cr — gain or loss? — LOSS ₹41cr; the ceremony promises truth, and tonight truth says decline. **10.** Neutral price? — ₹461cr: below it the price loses the argument with ₹677cr of derecognized carrying.

### 12.8 🎤 The Consolidation Forge — ten questions, no notes

**1.** What IS a group, in one line? — One creature wearing many legal skins; economic unity over legal separateness (CS1). **2.** The three locks of control? — Power over relevant activities, exposure to variable returns, and the ability to USE power to move those returns — all three, simultaneously (CS2, Ind AS 110). **3.** Why does the investment line vanish? — It is merely a shortcut in the parent's books; consolidation replaces it with the child's actual assets and liabilities at 100% (CS3). **4.** Goodwill formula and tonight's fork? — Consideration + NCI − net assets at fair value: ₹152cr proportionate, ₹167.5cr full (CS4). **5.** Who is the Stepmother and what did she eat tonight? — The NCI: an ₹8.5cr share of adjusted profit, minus her ₹2cr dividend, walking from ₹62cr to ₹68.5cr (CS5). **6.** The four elimination families? — Balances, trading-with-UPP, dividends, and asset transfers with their scheduled reliefs (CS6). **7.** Why does goodwill never amortize? — An indefinite-lived premium is tested, not ticked; it surrenders all at once and never reverses (CS7). **8.** Equity door versus the cliff? — Family slide-trades re-attribute inside equity without touching P&L; LOSS OF CONTROL detonates the full ceremony — derecognition, re-measurement at fair value, OCI recycling (CS8). **9.** The associate's one line? — Carrying = cost + share of profit − dividends, the equity method's one-line consolidation, and only its received dividend is cash (CS9, CS11). **10.** The CTA honor drill — US wing year-2, net assets $13m → $15m, profit $2m, spot ₹74 → ₹79, average ₹76.5? — Two limbs: 13 × 5 = ₹65cr on opening net assets, 2 × 2.5 = ₹5cr on the year's profit — a **₹70cr CTA credit** to OCI; translated net assets close at 15 × 79 = **₹1,185cr** (CS10).
