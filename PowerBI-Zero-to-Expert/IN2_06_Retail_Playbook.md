## 📘 Concepts

### 6.1 Direct vs regular — the quietest heist in finance
Every mutual fund runs two doors: **regular** (through a distributor, who is paid a trail commission inside the TER — typically ~1%/yr more) and **direct** (no distributor, TER lower by ~the commission). SEBI made direct plans mandatory in 2013; a decade later most AUM still enters regular — the heist survives on convenience and inertia. The arithmetic, canon: **₹10L lumpsum, 20 years, 12% gross → direct nets ~12%, regular nets ~11%**: direct wealth **₹96.46L**, regular wealth **₹80.62L — drag = ₹15.84L**, 158% of your original capital handed to intermediation one invisible basis-point at a time. On ₹1L/month career-scale SIPs the drag buys a second retirement home in Goa for someone else’s family. Doctrine: advice has value — pay for advice with a FEE you see (RIA), not a commission you don’t.

### 6.2 SIP mechanics — boring compound interest wearing a salary
A SIP is recurring deployment + time: it buys MORE units when NAVs fall (rupee-cost averaging) — the mechanism converts volatility from enemy into acquisition discount, PROVIDED the income engine (your salary) survives the bear. Honest truths: (1) SIPs don’t immunize losses — a 3-year SIP into a falling market shows red for years; survival is the feature, timing-immunity is marketing; (2) returns are measured by **XIRR** (money-weighted) — ₹10k/month for 20y at 12% ≈ **₹99L on ₹24L invested** (the 11% regular version: ~₹87L — there’s another ₹12L of drag); (3) **step-up 10%/yr** with salary growth roughly multiplies the terminal corpus — inflation-proofing the contribution is worth more than fund-picking heroics; (4) pausing SIPs in crashes is the single largest unmeasured fee in Indian retail — BF6’s playbook exists precisely for that month.

### 6.3 The product ladder — climb slowly, read the min bar
1. **Index funds/ETFs:** market return minus breadcrumbs (0.1–0.3% TER); the default core — IN2 3 explained the obedience machine you’re hiring.
2. **Active mutual funds:** you pay 0.5–2% hoping the manager beats TRI after costs — most don’t over decades; pick process, tenure, and rolling-window consistency, not last year’s trophy.
3. **Direct stocks:** your own FA7 verdicts — highest variance, zero TER, full behavioral exposure.
4. **PMS (₹50L min):** customized portfolios, fees ~2%+ profit-share — analysis grade required; tax-pass-through means THEIR churn appears on YOUR ITR.
5. **AIF (₹1 Cr min):** hedge/long-short/VC strategies — accredited territory; illiquidity, lock-ins, leverage: the ladder’s penthouse, entered only after the lower floors bore you.
Ladder law: the tier above never fixes a discipline gap below — it magnifies it.

### 6.4 Scam immunology — the checklist that saves careers
The modern fraud pack, with tells: **guaranteed-return schemes** (anything “2%/month fixed” — SEBI bars return guarantees for a reason: they’re uninsurable promises) · **unregistered tips/advisors** (verify on SEBI’s RIA/RA registry — a real registration number checks out in seconds; a screenshot of one doesn’t) · **pump-and-dump groups** (admin buys illiquid microcap weeks before “target ₹400 🚀”; you are the exit liquidity, not the community) · **leverage lures** (loan-for-trading, “funded accounts”, Dabba/bucket-shop apps settling outside the exchange machine — illegal in India, no depository, no recourse) · **impersonation** (cloned AMC/broker apps and look-alike advisors — always reach the institution from the OFFICIAL website, never from an inbound link). Scripted refusal, memorize the shape: *“Show me your SEBI registration and the audited net track record; I’ll verify independently and call you back through the official number.”* Fraud dies at verification requests; honest people welcome them.

### 6.5 Core-satellite assembly — the adult default
**Core (60–80%):** 1–2 index funds (NIFTY 50 + Next 50 or a flexifund), direct plans, SIPlined, step-upped — the compounding heartbeat. **Satellites (20–40%):** 1 active fund with a decade-deep process, an international/developed-market sleeve if it fits, maybe a gilt/TMF sleeve from FI5. **Stock sleeve (≤ caps from BF6: ≤5% single name):** your FA7 verdicts — where this academy’s skills are allowed to hunt. Rules stack: rebalance annually (bands 5%), no leverage ever for the core, every product bought only after its minimum-bar and tax-head are named. It’s unglamorous — which is precisely why it compounds while the glamorous donate tuition.
