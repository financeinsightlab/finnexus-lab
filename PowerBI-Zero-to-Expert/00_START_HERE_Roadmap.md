## 5. The 7 Laws of Mastery (read weekly)

1. **30/70 rule** — learn 30%, build 70%. Watching videos never made anyone an expert.
2. **Do every lab twice** — once following along, once from a blank file.
3. **Never paste DAX you can't explain** line by line out loud.
4. **Portfolio from day one** — screenshot every project; you'll publish them in Module 09.
5. **One deep article per week** — SQLBI.com is the gold standard; start after Module 4.
6. **Teach to learn** — explain each new concept to someone (or to me). If you can't explain it simply, redo the lesson (Feynman technique).
7. **Stuck > 30 minutes?** Use the 4-step rescue: ① re-read the exact error, ② reproduce it in a tiny test page, ③ simplify (smaller table, simpler measure), ④ still stuck — ask me and paste your formula + model screenshot description.
