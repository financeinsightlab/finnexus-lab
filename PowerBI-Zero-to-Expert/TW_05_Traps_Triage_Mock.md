## 📘 Concepts

### 6.1 The trap hall of fame
**TRAP 1 · ADD-THE-DAYS** — 12 + 18 = 30 "days together." The founding felony: rates add, days never. Antidote: **translate to flows before ANY arithmetic** — if you haven't said "units per day," you haven't earned the plus sign.

**TRAP 2 · MAN-DAY DILUTION** — believing the job's man-day bill shrinks when more bodies show up ("25 men ⇒ cheaper than 10"). Antidote: **man-days is the invariant** — headcount moves the calendar, not the invoice (canon: 150 man-days costs ₹3.75L at ₹2,500, whether 10 men or 25).

**TRAP 3 · LEAVE-LOSS MISREAD** — answering the leaver's post-exit solo time (8 days) when the project time was asked (12), or billing the leaver for post-exit units. Antidote: **two stopwatches on the page** — project's and the worker's — and the question's words circled before computing either.

**TRAP 4 · SIGN FLIP** — treating an emptier as filler (net 25 instead of 16 ⇒ 7.2 h instead of 11.25 h) or a leak as bonus flow. Antidote: **each flow gets its sign spoken BEFORE the table is summed**; then the never-fills sanity gate (net ≤ 0 can only drown).

**TRAP 5 · ALTERNATE OFF-BY-ONE** — fractional-cycle answers (20/1.5 = 13.33) or remainders credited to the wrong starter. Antidote: **whole cycles, then the day-walk, hammer named at every remainder** — "day 13 is A's" said aloud or not at all.

### 6.2 The 5-gate radar
1. **RATES** — every "n days" flipped to 1/n? Plus-signs applied to flows only?
2. **TANK** — LCM set? bank/credit bookkeeping phase-wise?
3. **SIGNS** — emptiers/leakers negative? net sign read as the verdict?
4. **CALENDAR** — alternates cycled + walked; leavers/joiners on two stopwatches; start-flip considered?
5. **SANITY** — crew < fastest solo ✓ · double bodies = half days ✓ · with-leak slower ✓ · man-days conserved ✓ — any answer breaking a smell-test returns to gate 1.

### 6.3 Triage
- **BANK (<25 s):** two-worker together-times (xy/(x+y)), man-day scalings, wage splits by flow, one-tap autopsies.
- **GAMBLE (25–70 s):** three-tap nets, leave/join book-keeping, alternate cycles with remainders, efficiency inversions.
- **DUMP (−0.25 exposure):** rotation×leak crossovers, multi-phase piecewise epics — unless one option falls to a smell-test (one elimination ⇒ guess-EV +0.167 per the PC-course law).
