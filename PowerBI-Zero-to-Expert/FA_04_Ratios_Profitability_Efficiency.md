# 🎯 FA4 · Ratio Engine Room II — Profitability & Efficiency (DuPont Inside)
> ROE is the headline; DuPont is the biopsy. ShopKart posts ROE 22.8% — is that pricing power, asset hunger, or leverage lipstick? The three-way split answers precisely: margin (4.73%) × asset-turnover (2.69) × leverage (1.79) = 22.8%. FA4 runs the decomposition machine, separates ROE from ROCE permanently, prices return-quality traps, and hands you the efficiency ratios that audit which square-feet and which salaries actually pay rent.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Decompose any ROE via DuPont (3-way and 5-way) and read the driver-story instantly.
- Discipline ROE vs ROCE: whose return is whose, when each lies, and capital-intensity contexts.
- Profile business models as margin-driven vs turnover-driven — and never compare across the two.
- Run efficiency radar: asset turns, inventory/receivable turns → days, store-level sales/sq ft, sales per employee.
- Detect return-quality traps: reflation reserves, buyback optics, negative-equity ROE explosions.

## 📘 Concepts

### 4.1 DuPont 3-way — the biopsy that names the surgeon
```text
ROE = PAT/avg equity = 13.25/58.1 = 22.8%
    = NPM (13.25/280 = 4.73%)  ×  Asset Turn (280/103.95 = 2.69)  ×  Leverage (103.95/58.1 = 1.79)
Read: ordinary margin, exceptionally velocity (2.69 turns!), civilized leverage 1.79
Story: ShopKart is a TURNOVER machine — thin margins sprinting fast shelves.
```
**Three surgeons, three cases:** a 22.8% ROE could equally be 12% margin × 1 turn × 1.9 (branded-luxury), or 2.5% × 2.2 × 4.1 (leveraged trader — the dangerous uncle). Same headline, different species: margin-ROE survives recessions, turnover-ROE survives thin margins, leverage-ROE survives until the refinancing wall — the last one gets a covenant file before an applause.

### 4.2 The 5-way upgrade (interview flex, tax/interest hunters)
`ROE = (PAT/PBT) × (PBT/EBIT) × (EBIT/Sales) × (Sales/Assets) × (Assets/Equity)`
= 0.749 × 0.887 × 7.14% × 2.69 × 1.79 → 22.8% ✓ (tax burden × interest burden × operating margin × turn × leverage). Each factor isolates one manager: CFO-tax (0.749), treasurer (0.887), operations, asset-planning, capital-structure. **Promotion-material:** dissecting a rival's ROE into 'their treasurer's gear vs their operations' engine' wins case competitions; then read ROCE separately for the operations-only truth.

### 4.3 ROCE discipline — judge the factory, exclude the financing
ROCE = EBIT / capital employed = 20/67.4 = **29.7%** (canon) — operating pounds per rupee of long-term capital, financing-agnostic. Marriage rules: ROCE vs WACC (12.6%) decides value creation (CF1's spread +17.1pp); ROE vs Ke (13.6%) decides equity satisfaction. **When ROE > ROCE, ask loudly whose leverage amplified it** (here: modest 1.79 multiplier — civilized); when ROCE > ROE, financing is DILUTING owners' share of a good factory (over-equity or loss-shielded debt costs).

### 4.4 Model profilers — margin vs turnover dialects
- Margin machines: luxury brands, SaaS, pharma (GM 60–90%, low turns) — fragile to price wars, adored by quality screens.
- Turnover machines: grocery, QSR infra, logistics (GM 10–30%, turns 2–5×) — fragile to execution-slips (shrink, spoilage, labor), adored by operators.
- Cheap hybrids: high-margin franchises with asset-light turns (ROIC stories planners tattoo). **Comparability commandment: NEVER rank a margin-machine against a turnover-machine on raw ROE — decompose first, then compare inside species.**

### 4.5 Return-quality traps — optics with accountants
- **Revaluation-inflated equity:** ROE 'improves' post-loss-year because reserves shrank; inspect the three-year equity path, not the headline.
- **Buyback-EPS/ROE lift:** fewer shares at same PAT (AC9's 11% illusion refueled) — legal, real, and still not operational improvement; compare ROCE flat-lines to stay honest.
- **Negative-equity ROE:** losses consumed equity → tiny/negative denominators print 300% fantasies — ROE with equity < threshold capital = unquotable; switch to ROCE + ND/EBITDA.
- **One-off sandwiches:** exceptional gains inside PAT (asset sales) parse ROE up; strip to core-PAT first, celebrate later.
