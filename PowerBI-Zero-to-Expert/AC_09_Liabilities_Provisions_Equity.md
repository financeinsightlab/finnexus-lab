# 🎯 AC9 · Liabilities, Provisions & Equity — The Claims Side of the Scale
> Every asset has a claimant — and the *texture* of those claims decides whether a company bends or breaks. Debt with a clock on it, provisions that are probable, contingencies that are merely possible, reserves that are confidence frozen in a line, and equity — the residue the owners actually own. Liabilities and equity is where finance grammar becomes capital-structure power: the language bankers, boards and acquirers negotiate in.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Classify liabilities by tenure (current vs non-current) and by certainty (debt vs provision vs contingent).
- Drive the **provision triad**: provision (probable + measurable → book it) / contingent liability (possible → disclose) / contingent asset (possible → stay silent).
- Decode equity's anatomy: share capital, securities premium, reserves (revenue vs capital), treasury/buyback moves, OCI glimpse.
- Price shareholder flows: dividends, buyback arithmetic (EPS accretion illusion), and the dividend-for-clarity doctrine.
- Answer the eternal interview: **debt vs equity** — cost, control, covenant, and why "debt is cheaper" is only half of a sentence.

## 📘 Concepts

### 9.1 Liabilities — the seniority staircase
Order of claims on liquidation (India-flavored reality): secured lenders (term loans with asset charge) → statutory dues (taxes, employee PF) → workmen dues → unsecured lenders & ODs → trade creditors → preference claims → equity. The staircase explains interest rates without any formula: **lower on the stairs = bigger risk = bigger expected return**. Equity stands last, which is why the P in P/E is tuition, not charity.

ShopKart's staircase (closing FY25, ₹L): van term loan 4 (secured on vehicle) · OD 15 (secured on stock/debtors) · payables 2.3 · creditors 26 (unsecured trade) · equity 63.4 absorbing every shock first.

### 9.2 The provision triad — the most-fumbled corner in exams
```text
PROVISION:      present OBLIGATION + PROBABLE outflow + RELIABLE estimate → BOOK IT (P&L + liability)
                e.g., delivery-accident case, likely settlement ≈ ₹1.2L → Dr P&L / Cr Provision
CONTINGENT:     POSSIBLE (not probable) or not reliably estimable       → DISCLOSE in notes only
                e.g., rival's trademark suit, risk assessed ~₹5L, lawyers say possible
CONTINGENT ASSET: possible INFLOW → stay SILENT until virtually certain (prudence asymmetry!)
```
The **near-miss distinction that interviews farm relentlessly**: a **reserve** is not a provision at all — it's profit set *aside* from appropriation (General Reserve, retained earnings) — a decision, not an obligation. The Companies Act-era distinction still matters in exam vocab: **provision is a charge AGAINST profits; reserve is an appropriation OF profits.** Same ₹-size, utterly different rankings: charge (above PAT) vs appropriation (below).

### 9.3 Equity's anatomy — read the ownership zone
```text
Equity = Share Capital + (Reserves & Surplus)
ShopKart: 5,00,000 equity shares of ₹10 = ₹50L    ← par/face value, an arbitrary slice label
        + General Reserve & Retained Earnings = ₹13.4L
        = Net Worth ₹63.4L
Securities Premium (when shares issue above par): issue at ₹40 ⇒ capital ₹10 + premium ₹30
   → the premium is EQUITY, not income — investors' over-par faith, banked in a reserve.
OCI glance: certain fair-value movements (some investments, re-measurements of gratuity plans)
   bypass the P&L and sit in Other Comprehensive Income, parked inside equity till settled.
```
Two anti-confusions drilled into every CA's bones: (1) share *premium* is capital-claim money (can't simply fund dividends freely — sections apply), and (2) par value is non-sense arithmetic (₹10); market price is the negotiated truth (ShopKart-equivalent trades at ₹30 in our P/E teaser ⇒ premium of ₹20 over par for fresh issues).

### 9.4 Dividends & buybacks — cash exits with personality
- **Dividend (₹2.65L paid, 20% payout):** a distribution decision from *distributable* profits; board proposes, shareholders approve. Doctrine fleets: cash-rich-no-projects ⇒ pay (trust); growth-hungry ⇒ retain (compounding machine: our RE went 2.8 → 13.4 *after* this dividend ✓).
- **Buyback:** company purchases its own shares — capital + premium exits, shares cancel, remaining holders own a BIGGER fraction. The **EPS-accretion illusion**: buyback at ₹30 := shares down 10% ⇒ EPS = PAT/shares rises ~11% *with zero extra rupee of operations* — arithmetic accretes while nothing operated. Compare the two exits: dividend pays ALL holders pro-rata (tax visible per shareholder), buyback pays only those who sell (price signal: "undervalued, says the board" — believe it when the insiders sit tight).

### 9.5 Debt vs equity — the real answer, in five sentences
1. Debt is cheaper because lenders stand higher on the staircase and interest is tax-deductible (shield = rate × tax).
2. Debt is also fixed-obligation: interest is owed in bad years, covenants are owed in all years (failing debt-service coverage ⇒ technical default even while profitable).
3. Equity is permanent, covenant-light, and forgiving — but the costliest form: it asks for growth AND shares control.
4. The optimal mix, levered-βs, and WACC's bluff are Corporate Finance (Course 2)'s crown jewels; today, own the vocabulary: **leverage = debts ÷ equity (ShopKart 19/63.4 ≈ 0.30 — conservative trade safety).**
5. A CEO's honest summary: *"Debt is cheap till it isn't; equity is forgiving till you need a payday."*

### 9.6 Covenants — the fine print that runs companies
Lenders attach terms: minimum interest coverage (8.7× ✓), max D/E (ours 0.30 vs a 2.0 ceiling – yawning headroom), security top-ups when stock values fall, and **event-of-default** nets for undisclosed related-party loans. Covenant braiding is where bankers quietly govern; a CFO's calendar has loan-review dates circled in a colour shareholders never see.

### ✅ Selected answers
1. EPS 7.5 → PAT 30L / 3,60,000 = ₹8.33 post-buyback (+11% arithmetic); board-line: "buyback preferred here because continuing shareholders demonstrated conviction while exiting ones were paid a premium; dividends do both groups in the same envelope."
2. (a) practice-based constructive obligation → PROVISION bookable (custom creates obligation) (b) probable outflow + estimable → book provision 6 (c) remote → no disclosure needed honestly (below even contingent threshold) — and the table is the whole point: three similar ₹, three different fates.
3. NW = 50 + 10 + 6 + 8 − 5 = **₹69L** — footnote: revaluation raises equity with VALUES not cash; credit-readers scale it down mentally; the revaluation reserve is barred from free-dividend use.
4. Secured 30 ✓; statutory 6 ✓; remaining 19 split pro-rata between trade (22) and unsecured (10): 19 × 22/32 = 13.06 and 19 × 10/32 = 5.94 ⇒ creditors recover 59%; equity 0. The staircase eats **bottom-up losses and top-down cash** — asbestos-law of commerce.
5. EBIT 20 (take equity = 100 for both): A has debt 150 ⇒ interest 16.5 ⇒ PAT = (20 − 16.5)×0.75 = 2.63; B has debt 20 ⇒ interest 2.2 ⇒ PAT = (20 − 2.2)×0.75 = 13.35. EBIT 10: A PAT (10 − 16.5) = loss −6.5; B PAT (10 − 2.2)×0.75 = 5.85. A's PAT swings −347%, B's −56% ⇒ **leverage amplifies BOTH directions** — cheap when the sail fills, catastrophic against the tide.
6. "A provision is a charge AGAINST profits for a present obligation with probable measurable outflow — say a ₹1.2L settlement estimate; a reserve is an appropriation OF profits kept for strength — say general reserve from surplus; charges rank above PAT; appropriations live below it. Same rupee, opposite constitutions."
7. Consistent high payout with humble ROE ⇒ the firm can't find projects beating its shareholders' outside options — the honest dividend; the WACC course will brand that as "returning capital cheaper than misallocating it" — an underrated act of governance.

## ❓ Quiz
1. A customer lawsuit with probable unfavorable outcome estimated at ₹1.2L should be:
   - (a) disclosed in notes only
   - (b) BOOKED as a provision — present obligation + probable + reliably estimable = Dr P&L, Cr Provision (possible-only or inestimable claims stay as contingent disclosures; and inflows stay silent until virtually certain)
   - (c) ignored until judgement
2. ShopKart issues 10,000 shares at ₹40 (par ₹10). The ₹30 premium per share lands in:
   - (a) the P&L as other income
   - (b) Securities Premium within EQUITY — over-par consideration is capital-claim money, never income; it strengthens net worth without a single rupee of operations
   - (c) a contingent reserve
3. Buying back 10% of shares with ₹15L cash while PAT is unchanged leaves EPS:
   - (a) unchanged — cash left
   - (b) ~11% HIGHER purely arithmetically (PAT spread over 10% fewer shares) — the accretion illusion: beautiful per-share optics with zero operational improvement; compare dividends, which never change per-share arithmetic at all
   - (c) lower — buyback cost money

### ✅ Answers
1. **(b)** — probable + measurable is the booking trigger; the triad exists precisely to stop "we'll see" accounting.
2. **(b)** — premiums are equity's applause section; booking them as profit is an old scam red-flagged in every fraud syllabus.
3. **(b)** — per-share metrics react to denominators as well as numerators; ask "WHAT operated better?" before applauding any optics.
