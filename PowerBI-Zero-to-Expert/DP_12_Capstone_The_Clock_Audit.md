## 📘 Concepts

### 12.0 The file at a glance

Meridian Castings Ltd, FY just ended. Profit before these adjustments: ₹68L. Fixed-asset events touched this year: a press commissioned 31 March, a crane running since 1 April, factory land revalued, a "plant upgrade" line, and a machine moved to the sale bay on day one of the year. Each exhibit below is presented as the BOOKS HAVE IT — you find the doctrine, the fix, and the first-year P&L contribution.

### 12.1 Exhibit 1 — the ₹59L press with a stowaway (DP2 doctrine)

**As booked:** packaging press capitalized at ₹59L on 31 March; available-for-use same day; depreciation starts next year. Inside the ₹59L: operator training ₹3L (the rest of the cage — net price, duty, freight, installation, testing, ₹2L provision — all clean).
**Doctrine:** training is the exclusion wall's oldest tenant — the asset works regardless of who can drive it; never attributable.
**Fix:** Dr training expense ₹3L; Cr PPE ₹3L. No depreciation effect this year (clock starts next April — the exhibit is engineered to isolate the wall, no fog).
**P&L effect: profit overstated ₹3.0L** (as booked: zero charge; correct: ₹3.0L expense).

### 12.2 Exhibit 2 — the crane in the trench coat (DP3 doctrine)

**As booked:** overhead crane ₹90L commissioned 1 April, single asset, 15-year life, straight-line, residual nil → charged ₹6.0L. Engineering component slips exist in the file: bridge & girder ₹36L/30yr, hoist ₹22.5L/9yr, drives ₹13.5L/9yr, runway ₹9L/30yr, controls ₹9L/5yr.
**Doctrine:** para 43 — significant parts, different lives, separate clocks: ₹1.2 + ₹2.5 + ₹1.5 + ₹0.3 + ₹1.8 = ₹7.3L. The composite ₹6.0L buries ₹1.3L of consumption.
**Fix:** re-register as five components retrospectively-applied-from-commissioning (estimate refinement of register structure, prospectively clean): additional depreciation charge ₹1.3L.
**P&L effect: profit overstated ₹1.3L.**

### 12.3 Exhibit 3 — the uplift that called itself income (DP5 doctrine)

**As booked:** factory land (cost-model policy class, never revalued) was "revalued" by the controller: carrying ₹40L → fair value ₹48L; the ₹8L credited to other income in P&L; no class election policy, no valuer's report filed.
**Doctrine:** a one-asset P&L uplift fails everything at once — elections run by CLASS; gains run to OCI and the revaluation surplus (net of deferred tax), NEVER to profit; the only P&L door is reversing that same land parcel's earlier P&L decrease (none exists here).
**Fix:** reverse the ₹8L from P&L; if management genuinely adopts revaluation for the land class (all parcels, policy note, valuer engaged), route gross ₹8L to OCI with ₹2L DTL (25% teaching rate) → net surplus ₹6L. If the class stays cost-model, land simply returns to ₹40L and the ₹8L evaporates.
**P&L effect: profit overstated ₹8.0L either way.**

### 12.4 Exhibit 4 — the paint job in the capex drawer (DP7 doctrine)

**As booked:** "Plant upgrades ₹2L" capitalized this year, 5-year straight-line → charged ₹0.4L. The invoice: repainting and pit-filling of press bodies plus annual servicing kits.
**Doctrine:** para 12's servicing wall — restores the status quo, buys no future benefit, fails all five enhancement tests.
**Fix:** Dr repairs expense ₹2L; Cr PPE ₹2L; reverse the depreciation charged ₹0.4L.
**P&L effect: profit overstated ₹1.6L** (booked charge ₹0.4L; correct charge ₹2.0L).

### 12.5 Exhibit 5 — the machine that kept its clock after the sale sign (DP8 doctrine)

**As booked:** the 7-axis mill (carrying ₹25L) was moved to the disposal bay on 1 April: board plan approved, broker engaged, priced at ₹26L fair, sale expected inside five months, costs-to-sell ₹1L. Depreciation of ₹2.5L was charged for the full year "because it technically still stood here." FVLCD ₹25L (26 − 1) equals carrying — no write-down triggered.
**Doctrine:** Ind AS 105 — criteria met on 1 April → classify held for sale, depreciation STOPS that day, measure at lower of carrying and FVLCD (25 vs 25 → stays ₹25L).
**Fix:** reverse the ₹2.5L depreciation; re-present as non-current held for sale. (Books as they stand UNDERSTATE profit.)
**P&L effect: profit understated ₹2.5L → correction adds back +₹2.5L.**

### 12.8 The 10-question interview forge

1. Walk me from purchase order to balance sheet: what climbs into a machine's cost — and name three impostors. (₹59L cage; wall: training, admin, abnormal waste.)
2. "Depreciation is valuation." Agree or destroy. (Destroy: allocation of cost over consumption pattern; valuation lives in 113-land or the revaluation model's OCI corridor.)
3. A crane is one asset. Prove me wrong. (₹90L five-clock canon; ₹7.3L vs ₹6.0L; ₹1.3L recurring understatement and the year-9 disposal reckoning.)
4. Derive me a 25% WDB rate without looking anything up. (1 − (S ÷ C)^(1 ÷ n) = 1 − (0.1)^(1 ÷ 8) ≈ 25.01%; rounding policy documented.)
5. Why does an upward revaluation never touch profit? (Para 39: OCI → surplus, net of deferred tax; anti-earnings-engineering corridor; P&L door only for reversing that asset's own earlier decrease.)
6. Sell me a revalued asset — where does the surplus go at disposal? (Gain on REBASED carrying to P&L; surplus walks surplus-to-RE inside equity, recycled never.)
7. When does capitalization of interest stop — twice? (Substantially-ready-for-use; and suspended during extended-development halts — ₹20L capitalized, ₹4L expensed canon.)
8. Repairs vs replacements — give me the two-step. (New slice in, old slice out: derecognize estimated-old-carrying ₹1.6L, loss to P&L; zombie-component ban.)
9. A CFO says "no depreciation while the machine waits for its buyer." Right standard, right month? (Ind AS 105: dep stops at classification date — criteria day, not sale day; lower-of measurement kicks in.)
10. Read this schedule: additions ₹45cr, charge ₹30cr, average age rising. Verdict. (Cover 1.5 vs age climbing — reconciliation tells whether expansion is real or the CWIP ageing note hides parked projects; five-minute drill, in order.)
