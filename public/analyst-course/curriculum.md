# SignalPath — Complete Business Analytics Curriculum

**Learning contract:** Every chapter follows: explain in plain language → analogy → worked example → guided practice → business case → check for understanding → portfolio/interview connection. The learner attempts before solutions are shown.

## Level 0 — Analytics Orientation (absolute zero)
1. **What Business Analytics Is** — decisions, data, information, insight, analyst value; distinguishes analyst, BI analyst, data analyst, product analyst, and data scientist.
2. **The Four Types of Analytics** — descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, prescriptive; map each to real retail and SaaS decisions.
3. **From Symptom to Decision** — stakeholders, decisions, business symptoms, measurable questions, metrics, constraints, and scope.
4. **The Analytics Lifecycle** — frame, collect, prepare, analyze, communicate, measure; why it is iterative.
5. **Data Literacy from First Principles** — rows, columns, records, variables, units of analysis, data types, grain, and data dictionaries.
6. **Metrics and KPI Thinking** — metrics vs KPIs, leading/lagging indicators, north-star metrics, metric trees, guardrails.
7. **How Analysts Think** — hypotheses, assumptions, correlation versus causation, evidence, prioritization, and decision trade-offs.
8. **Communicating Insights** — observation versus insight versus recommendation, executive summaries, uncertainty, and the “so what?” test.
9. **Mini-project: Retail Decision Brief** — learner frames a conversion problem, proposes metrics/data, analysis plan, and stakeholder-ready recommendation.
10. **Level Assessment & Reflection** — terminology, metric-tree exercise, case framing, and skills-gap plan.

## Level 1 — Spreadsheet Analyst
1. Spreadsheet workspace, navigation, workbooks, sheets, cells, and data types.
2. Clean data entry, tables, formatting, sorting, filtering, and named ranges.
3. Formula logic: operators, relative/absolute/mixed references and audit techniques.
4. Essential formulas: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, ROUND, IF, IFS, AND, OR.
5. Conditional aggregation: SUMIF/SUMIFS, COUNTIF/COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIFS.
6. Lookup and matching: XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, match types, lookup failure modes.
7. Text and date cleanup: TRIM, CLEAN, LEFT/RIGHT/MID, TEXTSPLIT, DATE functions.
8. Errors, data validation, conditional formatting, and control checks.
9. Pivot tables, pivot charts, slicers, grouping, and calculated fields.
10. Spreadsheet data cleaning and introductory Power Query concepts.
11. Dashboard design: audience, hierarchy, chart selection, KPI cards, interactivity.
12. Scenario analysis, sensitivity tables, break-even, and goal seek.
13. Projects: sales dashboard; e-commerce performance; HR dashboard; personal finance; marketing campaign; inventory analysis.

## Level 2 — Statistics for Business Decisions
1. Why statistics: population, sample, parameter, statistic, variability.
2. Data distributions, center, spread, percentiles, quartiles, IQR, and outliers.
3. Mean versus median; skewness and robust summaries.
4. Probability, events, independence, conditional probability, and Bayes intuition.
5. Sampling, selection bias, survivorship bias, sample size, and data-generating processes.
6. Normal distribution and the Central Limit Theorem in business language.
7. Confidence intervals: uncertainty ranges and correct interpretation.
8. Hypothesis testing: null/alternative, p-value, alpha, Type I/II errors, and power.
9. Correlation, covariance, confounding, and causality traps.
10. Regression intuition and interpreting coefficient, fit, and residuals.
11. A/B testing: hypothesis, metrics, randomization, duration, novelty, and guardrails.
12. Projects: campaign experiment, customer behavior analysis, churn investigation, experimentation memo.

## Level 3 — SQL Analyst
1. Databases, schemas, tables, keys, relationships, entity grain.
2. SELECT, aliases, DISTINCT, LIMIT, WHERE and predicate logic.
3. ORDER BY, NULL semantics, CASE, COALESCE, CAST, and safe filtering.
4. Aggregation: COUNT/SUM/AVG, GROUP BY, HAVING, and metric definitions.
5. JOINs: inner, left, right, full, self joins; row multiplication and validation.
6. Set operations, subqueries, correlated subqueries, and CTEs.
7. Date/time analysis, calendars, cohorts, and period definitions.
8. Window functions: partitions, order, ROW_NUMBER, RANK, LAG/LEAD, running totals.
9. Customer funnels, retention, segmentation, and subscription analytics.
10. Query quality: readability, validation, query plans, indexes, and performance concepts.
11. Data modeling: normalized operational data versus star schema.
12. Projects: e-commerce analysis; SaaS cohorts; banking segmentation; healthcare operations; advanced SQL interview pack.

## Level 4 — Python Analyst
1. Python environment, notebooks, variables, types, strings, collections.
2. Conditions, loops, functions, imports, errors, files, and reproducible notebooks.
3. NumPy arrays and vectorized thinking.
4. Pandas Series/DataFrames; load CSV, Excel, JSON, and database query results.
5. Inspect, clean, standardize, validate, de-duplicate, impute, and document data.
6. Filter, sort, group, aggregate, merge, pivot, reshape, apply, and manage dates.
7. Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly; charts chosen for a question rather than decoration.
8. Exploratory analysis, reusable analysis functions, and reporting notebooks.
9. Introductory scikit-learn: train/test, regression, classification, clustering, evaluation.
10. Projects: sales analysis; customer segmentation; campaign analysis; employee attrition; churn prediction; demand forecast.

## Level 5 — Data Quality & Preparation
1. Professional quality dimensions: accuracy, completeness, consistency, validity, timeliness, uniqueness.
2. Profiling, distributions, null patterns, duplicates, invalid categories and referential integrity.
3. Missingness mechanisms and defensible treatment choices.
4. Outliers, anomaly investigation, rules versus statistical flags.
5. Standardization, parsing dates, categorical mapping, and master data.
6. Validation tests, reconciliation, data contracts, data dictionary, lineage, and change logs.
7. Project: messy enterprise cleanup with a quality report and reproducible pipeline.

## Level 6 — Business Intelligence
1. BI workflow, semantic layer, facts/dimensions, grains, relationships, and star schema.
2. Power Query data import, transformations, refresh, and query folding concepts.
3. Power BI modeling: relationships, date table, measures vs calculated columns.
4. DAX foundations: CALCULATE, FILTER, context, iterators, time intelligence.
5. Dashboard UX, accessibility, chart selection, drill-through, slicers, tooltips, performance.
6. Tableau Public and Looker Studio equivalents.
7. Projects: executive sales; HR; finance; healthcare; marketing; SaaS metrics dashboards.

## Level 7 — Business Analytics
1. Business model and stakeholder analysis.
2. KPI design, metric trees, north stars, leading/lagging indicators.
3. Revenue, cost, margin, profitability, unit economics, pricing, and forecasting.
4. Acquisition, lifecycle, segmentation, LTV, CAC, retention and churn.
5. Funnel, root cause, Pareto, SWOT, 3C and Porter frameworks applied with data.
6. Scenario planning, recommendation design, risks, and impact measurement.
7. Projects: SaaS growth; retail profitability; pricing strategy; healthcare utilization; startup unit economics.

## Level 8 — Financial Analytics
1. Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, revenue recognition basics.
2. Gross/operating margin, EBITDA, working capital, ratios, variance analysis.
3. Budgeting, forecasting, financial models, assumptions, and auditability.
4. NPV, IRR, ROI, break-even, sensitivity/scenario analysis.
5. Projects: budget variance dashboard; SaaS financial model; company performance analysis; investment memo.

## Level 9 — Product Analytics
1. Product strategy, user journey, event taxonomy, instrumentation, metric definitions.
2. DAU/WAU/MAU, activation, engagement, retention, churn and stickiness.
3. Funnels, cohorts, feature adoption, segmentation and journey analysis.
4. Product experimentation, A/B test design, analysis, rollout and ethics.
5. Projects: mobile app activation; SaaS retention; feature launch; e-commerce funnel.

## Level 10 — Advanced Analytics
1. Regression and logistic regression for decisions.
2. Classification, clustering, trees, ensembles, features and leakage.
3. Time series, seasonality, forecasting, baseline models and errors.
4. Cross-validation, bias/variance, precision/recall/F1/ROC-AUC, calibration and business thresholds.
5. Explainability, monitoring, ethical limits and translating models into action.
6. Projects: churn prediction; demand forecast; lead scoring; fraud-risk triage.

## Level 11 — Analytics Engineering
1. Warehouses, lakes, marts, ETL vs ELT, batch and incremental models.
2. Dimensional modeling, slowly changing dimensions, and reliable metric layers.
3. dbt concepts: models, sources, tests, docs, lineage, environments.
4. Orchestration concepts, Git workflows, CI, data governance and observability.
5. Project: open-source ELT pipeline with tested transformation models and documentation.

## Level 12 — AI & GenAI Analytics
1. LLM fundamentals, prompts, structured outputs, tool use and limitations.
2. AI-assisted SQL/Python with verification and privacy safeguards.
3. Automated narrative reporting and insight quality evaluation.
4. Retrieval-augmented generation, semantic search and natural-language analytics.
5. Agent workflows, human review, hallucination tests, responsible use.
6. Projects: feedback analyzer; report generator; natural-language SQL prototype; research assistant.

## Level 13 — Case & Consulting Lab
1. Problem framing, issue trees, assumptions, market sizing and prioritization.
2. Profitability, growth, operations, supply chain, healthcare, finance, and product cases.
3. Data interpretation, synthesis, executive recommendation, and slide narrative.
4. 20 progressive cases from guided to ambiguous stakeholder requests.

## Level 14 — Production Portfolio & Career Readiness
1. End-to-end project standards: brief, dataset, dictionary, analysis, dashboard, report, README, presentation.
2. Git/GitHub, project reproducibility, documentation and professional review.
3. Portfolio narrative, resume bullets grounded in actual work, LinkedIn project summaries.
4. SQL, Python, Excel, BI, statistics, business, behavioral, and case interview simulations.
5. Five capstones: e-commerce intelligence, SaaS growth, healthcare BI, financial analytics, AI analytics.
